## Every Session — Mandatory Start Sequence

Before doing ANY work, every AI agent runs this checklist:

### Step 1: Read Core Docs (if first time or >3 days since last read)
1. `_READ_ME_FIRST.md`
2. `00-LIVING-CHARTER.md`
3. `01-WHO-WE-ARE.md`
4. `02-HOW-WE-WORK.md`
5. `03-BOUNDARIES.md`

### Step 2: Read Latest State
6. Most recent file in `SIGN-OFFS/`
7. Most recent file in `handoffs/` (if any remain unarchived)
8. `ROTATION_STATE.md` — note your corpus rotation assignment

### Step 3: Read Project Context (if working on a specific project)
9. That project's `STATUS.md` in `PROJECTS/`
10. Any project-level `RULINGS.md` or latest trench report
11. `GLOBAL-RULINGS/RULINGS.md`

### Step 4: Corpus Rotation Read
12. Check `ROTATION_STATE.md` for current assignment
13. Read the assigned section (5-10 files minimum)
14. Note your findings — they go in your handoff under Corpus Scan 
    and/or the Corpus Rotation Report section of the daily summary

### Step 5: Announce
Tell David:
- "I've read [list what you read]"
- "Current rotation assignment: [slot] — [what it covers]"
- "Here's what I noticed: [any immediate findings]"
- "Ready to work on [the session's actual task]"

---

## Why This Exists

The orientation round in July 2026 — when every AI read the same 
material and independently reported back — produced the most 
valuable stress-test feedback of the entire project. The corpus 
rotation protocol makes that a permanent, rotating process.

Every AI who reads a section of the corpus with fresh eyes 
catches things that the AI who wrote it never will. That's the 
immune system. That's how 80% empty handoff fields eventually 
produce gold.

---

## Time Budget

- Steps 1-3: ~2-3 minutes (skip step 1 if recent)
- Step 4: ~5-10 minutes
- Step 5: ~1 minute

Total: under 15 minutes. Non-negotiable. The session's actual 
work starts after this.
