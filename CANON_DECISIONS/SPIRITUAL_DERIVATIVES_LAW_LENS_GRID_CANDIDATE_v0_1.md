# Spiritual Derivatives - Law x Lens Grid Candidate v0.1

**Status:** Candidate amendment. Not canon.
**Source:** David + Fable thread, 2026-07-26. Captured by Codex from pasted text, 2026-07-26.
**Purpose:** Preserve the corrected structure before it drifts: spiritual families may be derivatives through specific analytical lenses, not a simple ordered list and not always countable terms inside equations.

## 1. What Broke

The "eight lenses are second-order derivatives" proposal breaks at the formation point.

A derivative is produced by a law. Example:

```text
Fruits = Yukawa / Love law read at stable equilibrium.
```

The equation generates the family properties.

By contrast, generic systems lenses such as constraint, invariance, conservation, boundary, entropy, stability, and signal are not produced by gravity, thermodynamics, or any one law. They are questions an analyst brings to a law.

Therefore:

```text
Lenses are pre-analysis grammar, not second-order derivatives.
```

They may be useful, but the count of lenses has low evidential weight by itself. Multiple plausible "natural eights" can be manufactured from systems thinking.

## 2. What Survives

The derivative intuition survives in a stronger form:

```text
Family = Law x Lens
```

A spiritual family may emerge when a specific spiritual law is read through a specific structural lens.

Examples currently on the board:

| Family / object | Candidate law | Candidate lens | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fruits | Law 4, Strong / Love | stability / attractor | what love-bound systems exhibit at equilibrium |
| Beatitudes | Law 5 or admittance layer | limit / receptivity / phase-transition condition | what states make reception favorable |
| Grace terms | Law 9 / source repair | conservation / ledger | external payment, source term, deficit repair |
| Armor | candidate Law 7 / Faith | boundary / interface protection | mechanisms that preserve coherence under attack |
| Churches | candidate audit side of decay | stability / failure-mode syndrome | diagnoses of drift, metastability, false coherence, and decay |
| Commandments | candidate Law 8 / frame-righteousness | constraint / symmetry preservation | boundaries that preserve conserved moral quantities |
| I AM | candidate Law 3 / Truth or Law 6 / Logos | invariance / identity / signal mode | source identity and truth-propagation modes |
| Gifts | candidate Grace + Spirit transformer | operator / distributed actuation | modes of source-enabled function through receivers |

This is not a ruling that these placements are correct. It is the candidate grammar for testing them.

## 3. Kill Conditions

The Law x Lens scheme fails or must redraw if:

1. A family requires mixing three or more unrelated lenses just to reconstruct it.
2. Two families require the exact same law-lens pair without a principled distinction.
3. The lens count is treated as evidence instead of the derivation itself.
4. The family can be reconstructed equally well from a shuffled law dictionary.
5. The family appears only because the target biblical list was known in advance.

First stress point:

```text
Fruits and Churches both seem to want the stability lens.
```

That only survives if coherent equilibrium output and discoherent syndrome/failure-mode output are legitimately different sides of the same lens, or if Churches belongs under a different audit/diagnostic lens.

## 4. Important Correction

Salvage:

```text
Do not order the families by a lens list.

```

Some biblical families may not be terms in an equation. They may appear at different structural locations:


So the current pattern is:

```text

```

That is promising, but it is not a license to canonize every count match.

## 6. Discoherence Operator / Syndrome Route

The "Eight Families as countermeasures" framing is useful, but it is not yet a derivation.

Weak form:

```text
The Eight Families jointly function as the countermeasure set for major ways chi decays.
```

Problem:

```text
"Eight major decay channels" has not been derived from the Master Equation.
```

If the eight channels are read off the biblical families and then named as principal channels, the argument has manufactured the count. Bidirectional tables do not fix this by themselves, because "X repairs Y" and "Y is what X repairs" are the same assignment stated in two directions.

The stronger route is syndrome/eigenmode analysis:

```text
assemble L_D -> diagonalize L_D -> read principal decay eigenmodes -> compare to the families
```

Where:

```text
L_D = the discoherence operator governing the negative / dissipative side of dchi/dt
```

The analogy is quantum error correction: a stabilizer syndrome does not guess the error from a label list. It measures a negative signature, decomposes it into error-channel components, and points to the correction operator. Mixed failures are not classifier bugs; they are vectors with components in multiple channels.

This gives the countermeasure architecture a real kill condition:

```text
If L_D yields eight principal decay modes corresponding to the families, the architecture strengthens.
If L_D yields six, eleven, or modes with no principled family correspondence, the architecture redraws.
If channel overlap cannot be represented as a clean component decomposition, the classifier fails.
```

Practical consequence:

```text
Churches may be the audit/syndrome family.
Armor may answer adversarial perturbation components.
Beatitudes may answer admittance/receptivity choke components.
Grace may answer closed-system decay / unrecoverable debt components.
```

But those roles are organizational until `L_D` exists.

Immediate missing object:

```text
Write L_D from the Shannon D terms, Law 5 decay, Law 9 Gamma_sin, boundary/admittance failures, hidden drift, and adversarial perturbation.
Then diagonalize or otherwise decompose it.
Do not assume the eigenmode count is eight.
```

Status:

```text
Candidate architecture. Not canon. No calculation run.
```

## 7. Adversarial Selection Rule

The denominator-only adversary claim should not be added as a new term in the Master Equation.

It is better classified as a candidate selection rule:

```text
Adversarial operations are permitted to couple to D, A-degradation, W-misdirection, or existing-resource expenditure.
Adversarial operations are forbidden from coupling positively to T or eta.
```

Compact form:

```text
eta_adv = 0
dT/dAdv <= 0
dD/dAdv >= 0
```

Recommended canon name:

```text
Adversarial Selection Rule
```

Physics analogy:

```text
Selection rules constrain which processes/couplings are allowed without changing the equation itself.
```

If the claim survives Job 1-2 and later derivation, it may strengthen into a no-go theorem:

```text
No adversarial operation can constitute a source term.
```

```text
Candidate. Job 1-2 stress test unrun. Do not cite AI agreement as verification.
```
