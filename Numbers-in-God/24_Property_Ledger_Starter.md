# 24-Property Ledger Starter

Purpose: turn the 24-properties argument from a powerful intuition into a disciplined claim stack.

Claim levels:

- HARD_REQUIREMENT: physics/math genuinely need this in a strong sense.
- METAPHYSICAL_BRIDGE: serious philosophy needed; not merely physics.
- THEOLOGICAL_READING: meaningful Christian interpretation; do not present as neutral proof.
- ANALOGY: useful but not load-bearing.
- OVERCLAIM_RISK: needs rewrite before public use.

## Immediate Rewrite Rule

Replace:

> all 24 are structurally required for physics

With:

> the 24 properties form a layered witness: some are hard conditions for physics, some are metaphysical pressures created by mathematics, and some are theological bridge readings that Christian theism explains with unusual economy.

## Strongest Surviving Thesis

The 24-property pattern is not a formal proof yet. It is a structured abductive bridge: mathematics bears many features classical theism expects from a world created through Logos, and physics depends on enough of those features that the pattern deserves serious explanation.
