# 1. Composition Test On Cluster 1 Modal

Cluster 1 Modal:

1. Necessary
2. Eternal
3. Immutable
4. Self-existent
5. Invariant
6. Transcendent
7. Objective

Test:

```text
Does God -> Physics equal God -> Math -> Physics?
```

Composition condition:

```text
G2P = M2P ∘ G2M
```

## 1.1 Necessary

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God is metaphysically necessary. Therefore physics, as created order, depends on a necessary source. Physics has intelligible lawlike order because its source cannot fail to be.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

God's necessary rational nature grounds necessary mathematical truth. Physics is expressible through mathematics, so physics inherits lawlike necessity through mathematical structure.

### Convergence

Partial only.

The direct path yields ontological dependence on necessary source. The mediated path yields formal necessity or mathematical invariance. Physics itself may operate under contingent but stable laws. These are not identical.

Result: `COMMUTES_BRIDGE`.

## 1.2 Eternal

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God is eternal, outside time. Physics exists in time and is sustained by the eternal God.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

Mathematical truths are timeless or not time-indexed. Physics uses time-stable equations or laws.

### Convergence

Fails strictly.

God's eternity is not the same as timeless mathematical truth, and neither is the same as physical law stability. Physics can discuss time-dependent laws, emergent laws, symmetry breaking, or regimes without denying all physics.

Result: `FAILS_STRICT`; at most `COMMUTES_BRIDGE`.

## 1.3 Immutable

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God's unchanging nature grounds a reliable, stable created order.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

Mathematical truths do not drift. Physics depends on stable formal relations and repeatable lawlike structure.

### Convergence

This is one of the stronger rows. The shared abstract role is stability under relevant change.

But divine immutability is metaphysical. Mathematical immutability is truth-stability. Physical immutability is regime-stability or repeatability.

Result: `COMMUTES_CLOSE` if weakened to stability; otherwise `COMMUTES_BRIDGE`.

## 1.4 Self-Existent

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God is a se, uncaused, from Himself. Physics depends on God and is not self-existent.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

Mathematics is treated as independent of human minds or physical instantiation. Physics depends on math for formal expression.

### Convergence

Fails.

The direct path says God alone is self-existent and physics is dependent. The mediated path risks treating mathematics as quasi-self-existent. That creates a theological hazard: mathematics could become a rival eternal object unless divine conceptualism is explicitly used.

Physics does not require self-existent laws in the same sense.

Result: `FAILS`.

## 1.5 Invariant

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God is the same in nature; creation reflects stable order.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

Mathematics preserves structure under transformations. Physics uses invariance principles such as Lorentz invariance, gauge invariance, and covariance.

### Convergence

Strongest Modal row after objectivity.

Still not strict identity, because divine sameness is not a technical symmetry group. But the structural role is close: preservation under transformation.

Result: `COMMUTES_CLOSE`.

## 1.6 Transcendent

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God transcends creation as Creator. Physics is not God and depends on what transcends it.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

Mathematics is abstract / non-physical; physical laws are formulated through abstract structures not reducible to particular physical tokens.

### Convergence

Bridge only.

God's transcendence means Creator/creature distinction. Mathematical transcendence means non-reducibility to physical marks or instances. Physical "transcendence" of laws is philosophy-of-laws language, not a physics requirement by itself.

Result: `COMMUTES_BRIDGE`.

## 1.7 Objective

### Direct Path: God -> Physics

God's truth and being are independent of creaturely opinion; creation has objective order.

### Mediated Path: God -> Math -> Physics

Mathematical truth is independent of preference; physics requires public, observer-independent or at least intersubjectively stable results.

### Convergence

Strong.

The definition still shifts by domain, but the structural role is highly preserved: not preference-dependent.

Result: `COMMUTES_CLOSE`.

# 2. Property #25 Attack: Unitarity / Information Preservation

Gemini claims Property #25, Unitarity / Information Preservation, is a novel prediction.

Codex attack question:

> Can #25 be derived from a non-theistic source?

Answer:

```text
Yes.
```

Therefore #25 is **not novel as a structural property**.

## Non-Theistic Derivation A — Quantum Mechanics

Closed quantum systems are standardly postulated to evolve unitarily. A unitary operator preserves inner products and norm, which preserves total probability and makes evolution reversible in the relevant formal sense.

Source trail:

- Nielsen and Chuang, *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*, postulates closed-system unitary evolution.
- MIT OpenCourseWare quantum mechanics lectures treat time evolution through the Schrodinger equation and unitary operators.
- Parker, "Unitary Time Evolution in Quantum Mechanics is a Stronger Physical Postulate than Linear Time Evolution," argues unitarity is a physical postulate, not a theological one.

Derivation:

```text
closed quantum system
-> state vector in Hilbert space
-> time evolution by unitary operator U
-> U†U = I
-> inner products / norm preserved
-> probability conserved
-> reversible information-bearing structure
```

No theism required.

## Non-Theistic Derivation B — Hamiltonian Mechanics / Liouville

Classical Hamiltonian systems preserve phase-space volume under time evolution. Liouville's theorem gives measure preservation in phase space for conservative Hamiltonian flows.

Derivation:

```text
Hamiltonian system
-> symplectic flow
-> phase-space volume preservation
-> no compression/loss of ensemble density under ideal dynamics
-> classical analogue of information preservation
```

No theism required.

## Non-Theistic Derivation C — Reversible Computation

Reversible computation studies logically reversible operations where outputs preserve enough information to recover inputs. This is tied to Landauer-style thermodynamic cost of irreversible erasure.

Derivation:

```text
logical reversibility
-> one-to-one state transition
-> prior state recoverable from later state
-> information preserved under ideal computation
```

No theism required.

## #25 Novelty Verdict

Property #25 is not novel in:

- physics;
- quantum theory;
- information theory;
- reversible computation;
- Hamiltonian mechanics.

It may still be novel in a narrower sense:

> The prediction that a complete God/Math/Physics property list should include unitarity/information preservation as a 25th triadic bridge property may be original to this framework.

But that is not the same as discovering the property.

Canon-safe wording:

> Property #25 is not a novel physics principle. It is a proposed bridge-prediction: if the 24-property framework is structurally complete, information-preserving / unitary structure should appear as an additional cross-domain property connecting divine faithfulness, mathematical reversibility, and physical conservation.

## #25 Risk

Unitarity is not universal across all physical descriptions:

- open quantum systems are described by non-unitary effective dynamics;
- measurement is not simply unitary in textbook formulations;
- dissipative classical systems violate Liouville-style preservation;
- black hole information remains a historically important pressure point around unitarity and information preservation.

Therefore #25 should not be phrased as:

> physics always preserves information in every sense.

Safer:

> Fundamental closed-system dynamics in major physical frameworks often impose information-preserving or probability-preserving structure; departures from that structure are treated as special, effective, open-system, or foundationally significant.

# 3. Negative Control: Programmer / Code / Computer

Purpose:

Test whether the same 7-cluster method can make an unrelated triad look like God/Math/Physics.

Chosen triad:

```text
Programmer -> Code -> Computer
```

Reason this is a hard negative control:

It has the same apparent grammar as the target triad:

```text
source/author -> formal language -> executing physical system
```

If the method makes this triad look too strong, the God/Math/Physics argument has a lottery-effect risk.

## Scoring Scale

- 0 = no meaningful match
- 1 = weak analogy
- 2 = bridge analogy
- 3 = close structural match
- 4 = very strong structural match
- 5 = near identity within this triad

This does not score truth of theology. It scores whether the 7-cluster pattern can be reproduced in an unrelated triad.

## Cluster 1 — Modal

Properties: necessary, eternal, immutable, self-existent, invariant, transcendent, objective.

Programmer:

- not necessary;
- not eternal;
- not immutable;
- not self-existent;
- may be outside the program but not transcendent in divine sense;
- can be objective relative to code but not metaphysically objective.

Code:

- can be stable;
- can be invariant under copying;
- can be objective in syntax;
- not necessary, eternal, or self-existent.

Computer:

- contingent physical system;
- mutable;
- local;
- dependent.

Score: `1/5`

Verdict: Modal cluster strongly distinguishes God/Math/Physics from the control triad.

## Cluster 2 — Logical

Properties: consistent, true, rational.

Programmer:

- can write rational code;
- can be inconsistent or mistaken.

Code:

- must be syntactically consistent enough to compile/run;
- can encode true or false statements;
- follows formal logic.

Computer:

- executes according to logic gates and physical constraints.

Score: `4/5`

Verdict: High false-positive risk. Logical cluster is not unique to God/Math/Physics. Any formal/execution triad will score well.

## Cluster 3 — Structural

Properties: order-giving, law-giving, unified, generative.

Programmer:

- authors code and imposes order.

Code:

- constrains what the computer does;
- can be unified by architecture;
- generative in outputs.

Computer:

- executes the constraints and produces behavior.

Score: `4/5`

Verdict: High false-positive risk. Structural cluster maps strongly to programmer/code/computer.

## Cluster 4 — Epistemic

Properties: intelligible, necessary for knowledge.

Programmer:

- intelligible as agent, but not necessary for knowledge universally.

Code:

- intelligible to programmers/compilers;
- necessary for knowing what the program does, not for knowledge as such.

Computer:

- behavior can be inspected/logged.

Score: `2/5`

Verdict: Partial analogy only.

## Cluster 5 — Ontological

Properties: immaterial, foundational, non-local.

Programmer:

- physical person, local, contingent.

Code:

- abstract pattern can be multiply instantiated;
- not identical to any one physical token;
- foundational for program behavior.

Computer:

- physical, local, not foundational.

Score: `2/5`

Verdict: Code gives a moderate analogy to mathematics, but the triad as a whole fails the God-level ontological claims.

## Cluster 6 — Evaluative

Properties: judging.

Programmer:

- can judge output quality.

Code:

- includes conditionals, tests, validators.

Computer:

- executes comparisons, branches, assertions.

Score: `4/5`

Verdict: High false-positive risk. The evaluation operator appears in many computational triads.

## Cluster 7 — Scale

Properties: simple, infinite, perfect.

Programmer:

- finite and imperfect.

Code:

- can be simple at the base and generative in output;
- not infinite except in theoretical/computational capacity;
- not perfect in real systems.

Computer:

- finite, error-prone, resource-bounded.

Score: `2/5`

Verdict: Partial analogy only.

## What The Negative Control Proves

It proves the method is not automatically discriminating.

Clusters 2, 3, and 6 are generic across systems with:

```text
author -> formal instruction set -> execution substrate
```

Therefore, the God/Math/Physics proof cannot rest mainly on:

- logic;
- structure;
- law/constraint;
- evaluation.

Those are too easy to reproduce.

The differentiating clusters are:

- Modal;
- Ontological;
- Scale;
- possibly Epistemic if stated universally rather than locally.

## What The Negative Control Does Not Prove

It does not defeat the God/Math/Physics thesis.

The control triad fails badly on:

- necessity;
- eternity;
- self-existence;
- metaphysical transcendence;
- infinity;
- perfection;
- universal foundation of knowledge;
- objective grounding of all rational order.

That means God/Math/Physics still has a distinctive profile if the Modal, Ontological, and Scale clusters survive strict audit.
