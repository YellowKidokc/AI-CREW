# CONTROL 3 - Logic -> Mathematics -> Physics

Codex negative-control ablation suite  
Date: 2026-08-04

## Control Purpose

This is not an unrelated control. It is the nearest non-theistic rival.

```text
logic -> mathematics -> physics
```

It removes God while preserving a formal source, mathematical mediator, and physical target. If this triad matches God/Math/Physics structurally, then God/Math/Physics cannot claim uniqueness at the structural level alone. It must win by directional grounding: why logic and mathematics exist, why they apply to physics, and why persons can know them.

## Boundary Pattern Audit

The boundary pattern is very strong:

```text
logic -> mathematics -> physics -> empirical feedback constraining formal theory
```

This is the serious rival to God/Math/Physics.

Where it breaks is not structure. It breaks, if it breaks, at grounding:

- Why does logic exist?
- Why is logic binding?
- Why is mathematics applicable to physics?
- Why can physical minds know necessary structure?
- Does this triad preserve moral authority, consciousness, and truth as more than useful behavior?
