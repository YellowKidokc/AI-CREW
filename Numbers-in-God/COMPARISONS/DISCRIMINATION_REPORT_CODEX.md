The structural identity claim is not yet discriminating enough because Logic/Math/Physics reproduces the pattern without God.

God/Math/Physics is stronger than Programmer/Code/Computer, Mind/Language/Culture, Information/Computation/Matter, and Artist/Music/Listener, especially in Modal + Ontological + Scale.

Can the proof still be powerful?

```text
Yes.
```

But the standard must be revised:

> The proof is not that no other triad can reproduce the seven-cluster structure. The proof, if it succeeds, is that only God/Math/Physics grounds the seven-cluster structure without leaving logic, mathematics, physical law, truth, mind, and knowability as unexplained primitives.

## Recommended Revision To The Proof Standard

Replace:

> The 24 properties are structurally identical across God, mathematics, and physics, and no rival account explains all seven clusters.

With:

> The 24-property structure is a convergence signature. It defeats ordinary negative controls in the Modal/Ontological/Scale region, but the nearest formal rival, Logic -> Mathematics -> Physics, matches the structure without explaining its ground. Therefore the final proof must be directional-grounding, not structural identity alone.

```text
A cluster only counts as discriminating if it outperforms structurally similar negative controls.
```

Add this rival gate:

```text
Any non-theistic triad that matches the seven-cluster pattern must answer the grounding questions:
1. Why does its source exist?
2. Why is its source binding?
3. Why does it transmit to mathematics?
4. Why does mathematics apply to physics?
5. Why can finite minds know it?
6. Does it preserve truth, consciousness, and moral authority?
```

## Final Codex Ruling

The current seven-cluster structure has teeth, but not enough teeth to claim full uniqueness.

It proves this much:

> God/Math/Physics is far stronger than ordinary authored, symbolic, cultural, informational, and aesthetic triads.

It does not yet prove this:

> No non-theistic structural triad can match the seven clusters.

The decisive rival is:

```text
Logic -> Mathematics -> Physics
```

Therefore the next paper should not overstate structural identity. It should say:

> The seven-cluster structure creates a convergence signature, but the decisive argument is directional grounding. Logic and mathematics can imitate the structure; only God can ground why the structure exists, why it binds, why it reaches physics, and why persons can know it.
