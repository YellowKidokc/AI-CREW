## Codex Ruling

The negative controls show the structural signature is real but not unique by
itself. The strongest rival is:

```text
Logic -> Math -> Physics
```

That rival can imitate much of the structure. The discriminating layer is not
mere structural overlap; it is directional grounding:

```text
why the structure exists,
why it binds,
why physics obeys it,
why finite persons can know it,
why truth is normative rather than merely useful.
```

So the current proof architecture is two-layered:

```text
Layer 1: structural convergence identifies the signature.
Layer 2: directional grounding identifies the source.
```
