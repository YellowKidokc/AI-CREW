**Contributor:** GPT / Codex  
**Program:** Bidirectional Proof Program

**Status:** MIXED — one defensible structural core, no three-row proof of strict identity

## 0. Domain lock

The result changes depending on what “Physics” names. This must be fixed before testing.

- **God (G):** the necessarily existent, personal, triune God of classical Christian theism.
- **Mathematics (M):** mathematical truths and structures, not marks on paper or human mathematical practice.
- **Physics (P):** the fundamental law-structure instantiated by physical reality, not every physical object and not every provisional human model.

This is the strongest fair formulation. If **Physics** means concrete physical reality as a whole, then “Immaterial” and “Non-local” fail immediately: concrete physical reality is physical and its events are ordinarily located in spacetime. Restricting the target to *physical law-structure* may save a structural relation, but that restriction must be displayed rather than hidden.

### Common predicate proposed for the cluster

> **Ontological-priority predicate (OP):** (x) is not constituted by any one local material instance and (x) supplies an explanatory or constraint condition for a downstream domain.

OP is narrower than divine immateriality, weaker than divine foundationality, and different from quantum nonlocality. It is therefore a candidate cluster-level relation, not yet proof that the three original words operate identically.

## 1. IMMATERIAL

### Test 1 — Define it identically

Candidate predicate:

\[
I(x) := x\text{ is not composed of matter-energy and is not identical to a material token.}
\]

- **God:** Classical theism affirms (I(G)): God is spirit and is not composed of matter-energy.
- **Mathematics:** Under realism or divine conceptualism, mathematical truths are not material tokens. The inscription “2+2=4” is material; the truth expressed is not identical to that inscription.
- **Physics:** Concrete physical reality does **not** satisfy (I). A physical law considered as an abstract constraint may satisfy (I), depending on one's ontology of laws.

**Ruling:** The identical predicate fails across God, mathematics, and *concrete physics*. It survives only after changing the third relatum from Physics to **the abstract law-structure used by physics**.

### Test 2 — Required, not optional

- **God:** Required within classical theism because a materially constituted God would depend on a material substrate and would not be the unrestricted ground of that substrate.
- **Mathematics:** Non-identity with physical inscriptions is required for token-independent mathematical truth. But a nominalist can reject abstract mathematical entities while retaining mathematical practice.
- **Physics:** Immateriality is not required of physical reality. Whether laws themselves are abstract, immanent, dispositional, or merely descriptive is contested.

**Ruling:** Not required in all three domains. Strict proof fails.

### Test 3 — Same operation

In God, immateriality denies material composition and dependence. In mathematics, it denotes token-independence or abstractness. In physical law-talk, it denotes non-identity between a law and any particular instance. These produce related consequences, but they do not perform the same ontological work.

**Ruling:** Structural analogy with a shared “not identical to a material token” core; not identical operation.

### Test 4 — Forward reading

From an immaterial rational ground, one can reasonably predict non-material rational contents and a physical creation ordered by those contents. But an immaterial author can also create non-mathematical artifacts, and immateriality alone does not entail a specific mathematics or physics.

**Forward ruling:** Compatible prediction, not a sufficient derivation.

### Test 5 — Backward reading

From token-independent mathematical truth and globally applicable physical laws, one may infer that descriptions of physical regularity are not exhausted by any one inscription or event. This does not uniquely identify a divine mind. Platonism, structural realism, immanent realism, dispositionalism, and nominalism are live termini.

**Backward ruling:** Does not uniquely terminate in God.

### Test 6 — Kill attempt

Counterexample: a nominalist treats mathematics as rule-governed symbol use and laws as economical descriptions of material regularities. Whether that position ultimately succeeds, it shows that the move from non-material truth to God is not forced by the predicate alone.

**Final verdict — Immaterial:** **FAIL as strict three-domain identity. RETAIN as a typed bridge claim about token-independence, with rivals attached.**

### Six-year-old version

The number two is not the ink used to write “2.” God is not made out of atoms. But the world *is* made of physical things. So “not made of matter” does not mean exactly the same thing in all three places. We can keep the useful resemblance, but we cannot call this row a completed proof.

## 2. FOUNDATIONAL

### Test 1 — Define it identically

Candidate relational predicate:

\[
F_A(x,y) := y\text{ depends on }x\text{ for its possibility, structure, or explanation, while }x\text{ does not depend on }y\text{ in that respect.}
\]

This is explicitly a **typed relation**, not a one-place adjective.

- **God:** (F_A(G,M)) and (F_A(G,P)) are the claims to be established under divine conceptualism and creation.
- **Mathematics:** (F_A(M,P)) says physical law-structure instantiates or presupposes mathematical relations.
- **Physics:** Fundamental physical states/laws can be foundational relative to chemistry, biology, and higher-level physical phenomena, but not automatically relative to mathematics or God.

**Ruling:** A single relational form can be used, provided the type of dependence is displayed. “Foundational” does not mean “ultimate” at every layer.

### Test 2 — Required, not optional

- **God:** Ultimate non-derivative foundation is required by the stated God-profile; otherwise the entity is not the proposed ultimate ground.
- **Mathematics:** Some mathematical structure is required by any quantitatively and relationally describable physics, but it remains disputed whether mathematics *grounds* physics or merely represents it.
- **Physics:** A lowest physical explanatory layer is not guaranteed. Physics could have an endless effective hierarchy, plural fundamentals, or structural relations without a smallest entity.

**Ruling:** Explanatory priority is required locally; a single absolute physical foundation is not established.

### Test 3 — Same operation

The common operation is asymmetric constraint:

\[
x\text{ constrains the possible states of }y.
\]

God-to-creation is proposed constitutive grounding; mathematics-to-physics is formal constraint/instantiation; fundamental-physics-to-higher-level-physics is realization or composition. The direction is structurally similar, but the dependence relation changes type.

**Ruling:** **Partial structural preservation**, not univocal ontological identity.

### Test 4 — Forward reading

If God is necessarily rational and freely creates an ordered world, then mathematical constraint followed by physically instantiated structure is a coherent forward expectation:

\[
G\rightarrow M\rightarrow P.
\]

The forward argument still needs a transfer rule explaining why this mathematical structure and this contingent physical realization follow.

**Forward ruling:** Strong candidate architecture; incomplete without selection and instantiation rules.

### Test 5 — Backward reading

Physics exhibits dependence relations and formal constraints. Moving backward supports a search for more fundamental structure. It does not by itself determine whether the terminus is mathematical Platonism, structuralism, brute law, an information ontology, or God.

**Backward ruling:** Supports a grounding question; does not uniquely answer it.

### Test 6 — Kill attempt

Countermodels:

1. **Humean mosaic:** laws summarize events rather than ground them.
2. **Immanent realism:** mathematical structure exists only in physical instances.
3. **Platonism:** mathematics is necessary and independent, with physics instantiating it.
4. **Structural realism:** relations are fundamental without a personal source.

The row survives as a dependency schema only if it can discriminate among these.

**Final verdict — Foundational:** **PARTIAL PASS as a typed asymmetric dependency relation. FAIL as proof that every layer is foundational in the identical ultimate sense.**

### Six-year-old version

A house needs a foundation, but the concrete under the house is not the foundation of every house everywhere. “Foundation” tells us what depends on what. God, math, and physics may form a staircase, but we still have to prove that this is the right staircase and that it really ends with God.

## 3. NON-LOCAL

### Test 1 — Define it identically

Candidate predicate:

\[
N_L(x) := x\text{ is not confined to one bounded spacetime region.}
\]

- **God:** Classical theism denies that God is spatially contained, although God can act at locations.
- **Mathematics:** Mathematical truths are not located at particular spacetime coordinates.
- **Physics:** Physical objects and events are ordinarily localizable. Physical **laws** are not located at one point; they are stated as applying throughout their domain. Quantum nonlocal correlations are a different technical meaning and must not be substituted for (N_L).

**Ruling:** The predicate works for God and mathematical truth. It works only for physical law-structure, not physics generally.

### Test 2 — Required, not optional

- **God:** Spatial confinement would make God dependent on a larger spatial framework, conflicting with the stated ground-profile.
- **Mathematics:** Location-independence is required for the same theorem to be true regardless of where it is written or considered.
- **Physics:** Relativistic physics strongly enforces local causal structure. Global applicability of laws is required for their ordinary universal use, but some laws may be effective, domain-limited, emergent, or phase-dependent.

**Ruling:** Non-local *location* is not required across the full physical domain. Universal applicability and relativistic locality must be kept distinct.

### Test 3 — Same operation

In God, non-locality denies spatial containment. In mathematics, it denies location-dependence of truth. In law-structure, it denotes repeatable applicability across locations. These share location-independence, but their consequences differ. Quantum entanglement does not repair the mismatch.

**Ruling:** Narrow shared predicate; different ontological operation.

### Test 4 — Forward reading

A non-spatial rational ground can coherently ground location-independent truths and laws applicable at multiple locations. Yet non-locality alone does not predict Lorentz invariance, field locality, a particular spacetime, or quantum correlations.

**Forward ruling:** General expectation only.

### Test 5 — Backward reading

The cross-location applicability of equations supports the objectivity of formal description. It does not uniquely imply a non-local mind. A Platonist, structural realist, Humean, or dispositional essentialist can accept the same observation.

**Backward ruling:** Non-unique.

### Test 6 — Kill attempt

The strongest counterexample is physics itself: locality is a central organizing constraint in relativistic field theory. If “non-local” is defended by appealing to quantum nonlocality while the God and mathematics columns mean “not spatially located,” the row commits semantic drift.

**Final verdict — Non-local:** **FAIL as written. RETREAT to “not confined to a single material token or spacetime instance,” explicitly restricted to truths and law-structure.**

### Six-year-old version

Two plus two is four in Oklahoma and on the Moon. The rule is not sitting in either place. But a rock *is* somewhere, and physics cares a lot about where events happen. So we cannot use “non-local” as though it meant one identical thing everywhere.

## 4. Boundary transformation table

### God → Mathematics

| Field | Cluster 5 ruling |
|---|---|
| **Preserved** | token-independence; rational intelligibility; asymmetric explanatory priority; non-confinement to a created material instance |
| **Lost / not transmitted** | personhood; active will; omnipresence as personal presence; aseity; causal agency |
| **Introduced** | formal abstraction; theorem/structure distinction; multiple axiomatizations; explicit inferential relations |
| **Forbidden** | a mathematical contradiction being entailed by a perfectly rational source [L, conditional on source profile]; mathematics becoming a second self-existent deity [M under divine conceptualism] |

### Mathematics → Physics

| Field | Cluster 5 ruling |
|---|---|
| **Preserved** | formal constraint; repeatable relation; applicability across relevant instances; quantitative/structural dependence |
| **Lost / not transmitted** | universal instantiation of all mathematical possibilities; necessity of each contingent state; freedom from physical dimensions and boundary conditions |
| **Introduced** | spacetime location; causal dynamics; constants; initial conditions; material realization; domain-limited effective regimes |
| **Forbidden** | violation of the particular formal structure actually instantiated [P within its validated regime]; inference from mathematical possibility directly to physical actuality [L/Scope] |

### Cluster-level composition test

The indirect path preserves **constraint without material-token identity**:

\[
G\xrightarrow{\text{rational content}}M
\xrightarrow{\text{physical instantiation}}P.
\]

The direct God → Physics claim must yield the same result: a contingent, localizable creation governed by non-token-bound rational constraints. This is coherent, but it remains compatible with rival ontologies until the direction and source are independently discriminated.

## 5. Cluster 5 final ruling

| Property | Strict identity | Structural salvage | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Immaterial | FAIL | token-independence of truth/law | remove from strict three-domain identity table; retain as typed bridge |
| Foundational | PARTIAL | asymmetric explanatory priority | retain only with dependence types shown |
| Non-local | FAIL as written | not confined to one token/location | rename and restrict; never use quantum nonlocality as the bridge |

**Cluster verdict:** Cluster 5 does not establish full structural identity under the program's standard. It yields a defensible weaker invariant:

> Mathematical truth and physical law-structure are not exhausted by any one material token or local instance, and they occupy asymmetric explanatory roles. A divine-grounding interpretation explains this pattern, but is not uniquely forced by the pattern alone.

That result should be preserved. The three original adjectives should not be counted as three independent successful proof channels.
