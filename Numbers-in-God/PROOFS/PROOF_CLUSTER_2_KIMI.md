## PROPERTY 1: CONSISTENT

### 1. Identical Definition Across Domains

**God:** God cannot contradict His own nature. Two contradictory propositions about God's essential attributes cannot both obtain. This is not a limitation on God; it is the coherence of perfect being. A being that both is and is not loving is not a different kind of God — it is not God at all.

**Mathematics:** A formal system is consistent iff no well-formed statement and its negation are both theorems. `¬(P ∧ ¬P)` is derivable in classical and intuitionistic systems alike. Inconsistent systems collapse via the principle of explosion: `⊥ → Q` for any Q, rendering the system informationally void.

**Physics:** A physical theory is internally consistent iff its governing equations do not yield contradictory predictions for the same initial conditions. GR and QM are each internally consistent; their mutual inconsistency is the "fracture" the framework identifies as fallenness, not a failure of consistency per se.

**Predicate identity:** Non-contradiction of structure. In all three domains, permitting contradictions causes collapse into triviality or meaninglessness. The failure mode is identical.

### 2. Required, Not Optional

**God:** Inconsistency dissolves the concept. If "God exists" and "God does not exist" are both true, the term "God" becomes semantically empty.

**Math:** Inconsistent systems prove every proposition. Information content drops to zero. Consistency is the minimum bar for a formal system to discriminate between true and false statements.

**Physics:** Inconsistent theories make contradictory predictions. Experiment cannot adjudicate between them. The scientific method stalls.

### 3. Same Operational Structure

In all three: inconsistency → explosion of content → loss of discriminative power → system death. The mechanism is not analogous; it is the same structural failure mode operating at different layers.

### 4. Forward Read

If God is consistent, then the language He authors (math) must be consistent. If math is consistent, then physical theories built from it must be consistent. Prediction: we should find consistent math and consistent physics. Observation: yes.

### 5. Backward Read

Physics requires consistent math. Math requires a consistent ground. Consistency cannot be derived from inconsistency; it cannot emerge from chaos because emergence itself requires stable rules. The arrow points back to a consistent source.

### 6. KILL IT — Adversarial Attempts

**Attempt 1:** *God transcends logic; He is "beyond" consistency.*  
**Result:** Dead. "Beyond logic" means "both P and ¬P are true about God." The term "God" becomes semantically empty. Transcendence of logic is not a bigger God — it is a vapor. You do not get mystery; you get silence.

**Attempt 2:** *Paraconsistent logics exist. Math doesn't need classical consistency.*  
**Result:** Dead. Paraconsistent logics *control* inconsistency. They build fences around it. The fences are the consistency. The project is still: prevent explosion. You cannot build a paraconsistent system from pure inconsistency.

**Attempt 3:** *Physics might be inconsistent and we just haven't found the contradiction yet.*  
**Result:** Dead. Every successful experiment is a consistency check. The fact that we can do experiments at all — that predictions match observations — is evidence of consistency. If physics were genuinely inconsistent, it would not yield reproducible results.

**Attempt 4:** *You're defining God to match math. Circular.*  
**Result:** Wounded but not killed. The forward prediction breaks the circle: if God were inconsistent, math should be inconsistent. But math is consistent. The match is not merely definitional; it is predictive.

**Verdict:** SURVIVES. Cannot be killed without killing meaning itself.

## PROPERTY 2: TRUE

### 1. Identical Definition Across Domains

**God:** God is Truth — not merely truthful, but Truth itself. "I am the way, the truth, and the life." God's statements correspond to reality because God's nature constitutes the standard of correspondence.

**Mathematics:** A mathematical statement is true iff it corresponds to the structure it describes. `2 + 2 = 4` is true in ℕ because the successor function yields that result under the Peano axioms. Truth = structural correspondence.

**Physics:** A physical theory is true iff its predictions correspond to measured reality. Truth = empirical correspondence.

**Predicate identity:** Correspondence to reality. In God, it is ontological (He IS the standard). In math, it is structural. In physics, it is empirical. But the *relation* is identical: proposition ↔ reality.

### 2. Required, Not Optional

**God:** If God is not Truth, revelation is unreliable. Faith becomes wish. The concept of a being worth trusting collapses.

**Math:** If mathematical statements are not true, engineering fails. Bridges fall. Computers do not compute. Math becomes fiction with consequences.

**Physics:** If physical theories are not true (correspondent to reality), science is storytelling. Technology is luck.

### 3. Same Operational Structure

In all three: truth is the alignment between representation and reality. The representation can be verbal (God's word), symbolic (math), or theoretical (physics). The alignment is the same structural relation. When it fails, the system fails.

### 4. Forward Read

If God is Truth, then the language He authors must be truth-bearing. Math must have true statements. Physics must be describable by true theories. Prediction: yes. Observation: yes.

### 5. Backward Read

Physics requires true theories. True theories require a truth-capable language (math). Truth-capable language requires a truth-ground. Matter does not assert propositions. Convention can be false. The arrow points back to a ground that IS truth.

### 6. KILL IT — Adversarial Attempts

**Attempt 1:** *Truth is coherence, not correspondence.*  
**Result:** Dead. Coherence without correspondence is a beautiful lie. A perfectly coherent story about unicorns is still false. Physics does not care about internal coherence — it cares about prediction. Correspondence is the hard constraint.

**Attempt 2:** *Math is just formal game-playing; no truth claims.*  
**Result:** Dead. Formalism is a methodology, not an ontology. When you use math to build a bridge, you are betting your life on correspondence. The formalist who steps onto the bridge is a correspondence theorist in practice.

**Attempt 3:** *"God is Truth" is metaphor.*  
**Result:** Dead. Then God is not the ground of truth. Something else is. If nothing else is, truth is ungrounded. If something else is, that something is God by another name.

**Attempt 4:** *Tarski's undefinability theorem blocks this.*  
**Result:** Wounded. Tarski shows truth cannot be defined *within* a sufficiently rich formal system. But this proof operates across domains, not within one. God's truth is not a formal definition within math; it is the ontological ground of math's capacity for correspondence. The objection is a category error.

**Verdict:** SURVIVES. Correspondence is inescapable.

## PROPERTY 3: RATIONAL

### 1. Identical Definition Across Domains

**God:** God is Logos — reason itself. Rationality is an attribute of God's nature. He thinks, and His thinking is perfect coherence.

**Mathematics:** Math is the discipline of pure reason. Proof is rational demonstration. Every theorem is a chain of rational inferences from axioms.

**Physics:** Physics is the rational description of nature. The universe is rationally intelligible — it yields to reason. This is Wigner's "unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics."

**Predicate identity:** Intelligibility through structured reason. In God, it is ontological. In math, it is methodological. In physics, it is descriptive. The property is the same: reality is accessible to structured thought.

### 2. Required, Not Optional

**God:** If God is not rational, creation is not rationally ordered. The universe is chaos. It is not.

**Math:** If math is not rational, it is not math. It is random symbol manipulation.

**Physics:** If physics is not rational, prediction is impossible. Science is impossible.

### 3. Same Operational Structure

In all three: rationality = the capacity for structured inference from premises to conclusions. God's thoughts are rational. Math formalizes rational structure. Physics discovers that nature follows rational structure. Same structure, three layers.

### 4. Forward Read

If God is Logos, creation should be rationally ordered. Math should work as a tool for describing it. Physics should be possible. Prediction: a rational universe. Observation: yes.

### 5. Backward Read

Physics is rationally intelligible. That intelligibility requires a rational language (math). That language requires a rational ground. Irrationality cannot generate rationality; matter does not infer. The arrow points back to Logos.

### 6. KILL IT — Adversarial Attempts

**Attempt 1:** *The universe only appears rational because we're rational — anthropomorphic projection.*  
**Result:** Dead. Then why does the projection *work*? Why do predictions succeed across cultures, across species (bees do math), across independent discovery (calculus by Newton and Leibniz)? The match is too good for projection. Projection does not build bridges.

**Attempt 2:** *Quantum mechanics shows nature is irrational.*  
**Result:** Dead. QM is non-classical, not irrational. It follows Hilbert space mathematics perfectly. The math is rational. The physics is rational. It is counter-intuitive, not illogical. "Weird" ≠ "irrational."

**Attempt 3:** *Rationality is emergent from complex systems.*  
**Result:** Dead. Emergence requires stable rules. Stable rules require rational structure. You cannot get rationality from irrationality any more than you can get consistency from inconsistency. The ground must already be rational.

**Attempt 4:** *Evolution explains why we find the universe rational — it's adaptive, not true.*  
**Result:** Wounded but not killed. This is Hoffman's FBT theorem, properly scoped to *perceptual strategies*. But math and abstract reasoning are not perceptual strategies. The FBT theorem does not apply to formal proof or metaphysical inference. Extending it beyond its domain is a scope error. See Ontological Convergence Framework review, Section 4.3.

**Verdict:** SURVIVES. Rationality is not emergent; it is foundational.

**Rational:** "Why does 1+1=2? Because it makes sense. Why does the ball fall down? Because gravity makes sense. Why does God make sense? Because He's the one who wrote the sense-book. Same sense everywhere."
