## The Thesis

Mathematics does not resemble God. Mathematics bears the signature of its Author.

That is not a metaphor. It is a structural claim with three legs:

1. **God possesses 24 classical divine attributes** — necessary, eternal, immutable, simple, consistent, universal, immaterial, foundational, self-existent, infinite, perfect, true, rational, order-giving, law-giving, intelligible, necessary for knowledge, invariant, non-local, transcendent, objective, unified, generative, judging.

2. **Mathematics exhibits the same 24 properties** — not by analogy, not by surface resemblance, but structurally. Mathematical truth is necessary, eternal, immutable, consistent, universal, immaterial, foundational, self-existent, invariant, non-local, transcendent, objective, unified, generative, and distinguishes true from false. These are not decorative features. They are what makes mathematics *mathematics*.

3. **Physics requires these 24 properties to function** — not as nice-to-haves but as preconditions for the possibility of science itself. Remove consistency and derivation fails. Remove universality and experiments aren't replicable. Remove invariance and relativity collapses. Remove intelligibility and Wigner's "unreasonable effectiveness" has no ground. Remove the evaluative property and measurement has no meaning.

The direction is constitutive: **God → Math → Physics.** God authors mathematics. Mathematics governs physics. The arrow never reverses. Physics does not author mathematics. Mathematics does not author God.

This is not an isomorphism. An isomorphism maps between peers. This is something else — something the literature does not have a standard name for yet.

**It is a dependency cascade with property inheritance.**

A novel bears the signature of its novelist — not because novel and novelist are "isomorphic," but because the created thing inherits the character of its creator. Mathematics bears the properties of its Author not by coincidence but by origin. And physics, running on mathematics, inherits those properties whether it acknowledges their source or not.

The scientist who demands that the laws be consistent, universal, invariant, objective, intelligible, and true is already standing on 24 divine attributes.

He is using the signature to read the book while denying there is an Author.

## Why This Is Not Isomorphism (and Why It's Stronger)

An isomorphism is a bijective, invertible, structure-preserving map between two mathematical structures. What David Lowe has identified is not that.

It is stronger for three reasons:

**1. It is tridirectional, not bidirectional.** Three domains — God, mathematics, physics — not two. No prior systematic attempt in the literature maps all three with structural correspondence across 24 properties.

**2. It is directional, not invertible.** The arrow runs one way: Source → Language → Structure. This is not a defect. It is the thesis. Reversibility would collapse the argument — it would make God and math peers rather than Author and authored.

**3. It is a grounding chain, not a correspondence.** The properties don't just match — they *propagate*. God's properties ground mathematical truth. Mathematical truth's properties ground physical law. Each layer inherits from the one above and cannot function without what it inherits.

The correct formal category — when formalized — is likely a **commuting diagram of property inheritance** expressible in category theory as functors between three categories (divine attributes, mathematical ontology, physical law), with the functor direction locked.

Until that formalization exists, the honest name is: **tridirectional property correspondence with a one-way grounding chain.**

The bridge classification table:

| Level | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Analogy | Surface similarity | Exceeded |
| Structural analogy | Shared pattern | Exceeded |
| Homomorphism | Structure-preserving, one direction | Not yet formalized |
| Isomorphism | Bijective, invertible | Wrong category (by thesis) |
| **Grounding chain** | **Tridirectional, directional, inherited** | **This paper** |

## The Platonism Move (Why This Cannot Be Dismissed)

The strongest objection to the 24-property correspondence is that the God↔Math column assumes mathematical Platonism — that mathematical truths are discovered, not invented; that they are eternal, necessary, and immutable in themselves.

Formalists and nominalists deny this. If math is just a symbol game, the whole table collapses.

But here is the move that converts the vulnerability into the paper's strongest argument:

**Physics forces the issue.**

Column three — "Physics requires it" — is not a Platonist claim. It is an empirical observation about what science needs to function. Physics requires its laws to be consistent, universal, invariant, objective, and intelligible. Physics requires mathematics to be trustworthy, stable, and truth-bearing.

The working scientist is already committed to a mathematics that behaves as if it is necessary, eternal, immutable, non-local, transcendent, and objective. Not because the scientist holds a philosophy of mathematics — but because the science doesn't work otherwise.

Therefore:

1. Physics requires mathematical truth to have these properties (column three — empirical).
2. A mathematics with these properties IS the mathematics of Platonist realism (column two — philosophical).
3. A Platonist mathematics with these 24 properties is property-for-property the ground described by classical theism (column one — theological).

The atheist physicist who insists that the laws are real, objective, universal, and invariant has already conceded the ontology. He just hasn't followed it home.

This is not an assumption of Platonism. It is an *extraction* of Platonism from the success of physics. And it leads directly to the God of classical theism.

## The Seven Independent Clusters

The 24 properties are not all independent. A competent critic will collapse them. We do it first — honestly — and the argument survives at cluster level where it cannot be deflated further.

| Cluster | Properties | What It Says |
|---|---|---|
| **Modal** | 1 Necessary, 2 Eternal, 3 Immutable, 9 Self-existent, 18 Invariant, 20 Transcendent, 21 Objective | Math is not contingent, not temporal, not changeable, not dependent, not frame-relative, not reducible, not subjective |
| **Logical** | 5 Consistent, 12 True, 13 Rational | Math is coherent, truth-bearing, and governed by reason |
| **Structural** | 14 Order-giving, 15 Law-giving, 22 Unified, 23 Generative | Math structures, constrains, coheres, and produces |
| **Epistemic** | 16 Intelligible, 17 Necessary for knowledge | Math can be known and must be known for science to exist |
| **Ontological** | 7 Immaterial, 8 Foundational, 19 Non-local | Math is not physical, underlies everything, has no location |
| **Evaluative** | 24 Judging | Math distinguishes true from false — the evaluation operator |
| **Scale** | 4 Simple, 10 Infinite, 11 Perfect | Math is parsimonious at the base, unbounded in reach, exact in truth |

Seven independent clusters. All seven are classical divine attribute clusters. All seven are required for physics to function.

The rival "science of structure" explanation handles the Modal and Logical clusters tolerably — any consistent structural language would look necessary and rational. But it does NOT explain:

- **Epistemic**: Why is the structure intelligible to finite minds? (Wigner's problem)
- **Evaluative**: Why does the structure discriminate true from false rather than merely existing? 
- **Generative**: Why does minimal input produce maximal output? (The unreasonable fecundity of axioms)
- **Ontological**: Why does a non-physical entity govern physical reality?

These are the four clusters where the rival compression fails. Christian theism handles all four natively: Logos (intelligibility), Judgment (evaluation), Creation ex nihilo (generativity), Spirit (immateriality governing materiality).
