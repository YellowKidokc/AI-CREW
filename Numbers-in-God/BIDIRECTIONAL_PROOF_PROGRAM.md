## WHAT WE'RE DOING

We are attempting the deepest claim this framework has ever made:

**The 24 properties of God, mathematics, and physics are structurally identical — not analogous, not corresponding, not resembling — and the evidence trail is readable in both directions.**

Forward: from God's nature → predict what math looks like → predict what physics requires.
Backward: from what physics requires → identify what math must be → arrive at what authored it.

Both directions land at the same place. Same 24 properties. One author.

This is the same logic as evolutionary common descent. Evolution reads shared DNA backward to a common ancestor. We read shared structural properties backward to a common source. Same method. Bigger claim. Harder proof.

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

This is not apologetics. This is not "God is like math." This is not philosophy choosing between five ontologies.

This is a structural proof attempt. It either holds or it breaks. We test it the same way we'd test any structural claim — with kill conditions stated BEFORE we look at the evidence.

## THE TWO CLAIMS

### Claim 1: Structural Identity
24 properties operate identically across God, Mathematics, and Physics. Not similar. Not analogous. Identically. Same predicate, same meaning, same consequences when violated.

### Claim 2: Bidirectional Evidence
The authorship arrow is one-directional (God → Math → Physics). The evidence arrow runs both ways. You can read forward from source to structure. You can read backward from structure to source. Both paths converge.

## THE TEST STANDARD

### For Each Property (all 24, organized by 7 clusters):

1. **Define it identically** across all three domains. Same word, same meaning. If the meanings diverge, the proof fails for that row.

2. **Show it's required.** The domain dies without it. Not optional. Not commonly assumed. Required.

3. **Show it works the same way.** Same structure, same constraints, same consequences when removed.

4. **Read it forward.** From God → does this predict what we find in math and physics?

5. **Read it backward.** From physics → does this lead back through math to God? Is there any other terminus?

6. **Kill it.** Find a counterexample. Find a rival explanation. If you can't kill it, it lives.

### The Twelve-Question Comparison (applied equally to our model AND to naturalism/evolution):

1. What exists initially?
2. Which starting properties are assumed?
3. What is inherited or transmitted?
4. What performs the transmission?
5. Why does transmission preserve these properties rather than others?
6. What genuinely new properties can emerge?
7. What predictions follow before looking at the target?
8. What evidence distinguishes the model from rivals?
9. What would falsify or substantially weaken it?
10. Does the explanation preserve truth, consciousness and moral authority — or merely explain behavior?
11. How many brute facts remain at the bottom?
12. Does the explanation remain coherent when applied to its own reasoning?

## KILL CONDITIONS (stated BEFORE testing)

Lock these now. No moving the goalposts after results come in.

### The proof PASSES if:
- All 7 independent clusters survive the six-point test above
- The property definitions hold identical meaning across all three domains (not equivocation)
- The bidirectional reading converges (forward and backward land at the same structure)
- No rival explanation accounts for all 7 clusters simultaneously without additional assumptions
- Removing any cluster changes the result (independence test)

### The proof is PARTIAL if:
- 4-6 clusters survive but some require analogical rather than univocal predication
- The bidirectional reading converges for the surviving clusters but not all 7
- A rival explanation covers some clusters but not others

### The proof FAILS if:
- Fewer than 4 clusters survive identical-meaning testing
- The property definitions require different meanings in different domains to work (equivocation)
- A rival explanation accounts for all 7 clusters with fewer assumptions
- The mappings were defined into the domains rather than discovered independently

### The proof is DEFEATED ENTIRELY if:
- Someone demonstrates that the 24 properties were selected BECAUSE they match, not discovered TO match (selection bias)
- The same method produces equally strong results mapping any three unrelated domains (lottery effect)

**Blast radius if the proof fails: LOCAL.** The individual laws, the Master Equation, the grace derivation, the Judgment Layer — all survive independently. The framework loses its unifying spine but keeps its individual results.

**Blast radius if the proof passes: STRUCTURAL.** Everything reorganizes. The 24 properties become the foundation. The Ten Laws and Master Equation become downstream. The framework has a ground it didn't have before.

Evolution is not the enemy. It's the comparison case. We run BOTH accounts through the same twelve questions.

Evolution covers four different claims. Only the first is biology:

| Claim | Ruling |
|---|---|

| The universe originated without God | Cosmology/metaphysics — different question |
| Evolution explains reason, consciousness, morality, truth | Philosophical naturalism — not established by biology |
