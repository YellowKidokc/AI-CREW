# ORIGIN OF THE 24 PROPERTIES
## As told by David Lowe, August 4, 2026

Around June–July 2025, roughly a week before switching to the Opus-era conversations, David asked multiple AIs (including Sonnet) to define God axiomatically — if you had to define God formally, what properties would you give? All the AIs produced lists. David collected them, merged duplicates, and winnowed. The result was approximately 24 properties.

David looked at the list and said: "These are math."

Sonnet disagreed.

David insisted. He then ran an experiment: he took the same list, stripped the theological label, handed it to the next AI as "properties of mathematics," and asked them to evaluate. Nobody objected. The properties were accepted as mathematical without question.

The correspondence was not constructed. It was discovered by swapping a label and watching nothing break.

That is the empirical origin of the 24-property thesis. Everything built since — the tridirectional structure, the grounding chain, the physics-requirement column, the Kolmogorov compression — is architecture on top of that single observation: the properties of God and the properties of mathematics are indistinguishable under blind evaluation.
