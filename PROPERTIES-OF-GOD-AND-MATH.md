# The Shared Properties

*24 Attributes. Three Domains. One Author.*

---

## The Setup

Your uncle says you can't put God into math.

He's right. You can't put the ocean into a glass.

But God put math into the universe. And math — uniquely among all languages humans have ever used — shares His properties. Not a few of them. Not metaphorically. All of them. Structurally.

And not just between two domains. Between three.

God has these properties. Mathematics has these properties. And physics *requires* these properties to function at all. Remove any one of them and science stops. The universe stops. Structure stops.

Twenty-four properties. Three domains. One direction: God → Math → Physics. God authors math. Math governs physics. The arrow never reverses.

That's not a bidirectional isomorphism. It's tridirectional. A higher category than anything the literature has attempted.

## The Original 24

### 1. Necessary

**God:** Exists necessarily. Cannot fail to exist. Not contingent on anything — aseity in the classical tradition. The one being whose non-existence is logically impossible.

**Math:** Cannot be otherwise. 2 + 2 = 4 in every possible world. Mathematical truths are not contingent — they hold necessarily, not by convention, not by agreement, not by force. They just ARE.

**Physics requires it:** If the laws could be otherwise, nothing is predictable. The entire scientific enterprise assumes its laws are necessary within this universe. Remove necessity and you cannot write a single equation with any confidence it will hold tomorrow.

---

### 2. Eternal

**God:** "Before Abraham was, I AM" (John 8:58). Outside of time. Not "very old" — not temporal at all.

**Math:** True before the universe existed. True after the heat death. π was irrational before there were circles. The Pythagorean theorem held before there were triangles. Mathematical truth does not begin and does not expire.

**Physics requires it:** Cosmology needs eternity somewhere. If the laws aren't eternal, they started. If they started, something started them. That thing needs to be eternal or you get infinite regress — which explains nothing. The laws being "coextensive with the universe" isn't an answer. It's a deferral. Noether's theorem: time-translation symmetry → conservation of energy. The eternality of mathematical truth is what makes conservation laws work.

---

### 3. Immutable

**God:** "I the LORD do not change" (Malachi 3:6). Not "consistent so far." Unchangeable in principle.

**Math:** Mathematical truths do not change. Ever. π has never drifted. The prime numbers have never rearranged. There are active searches for drifting physical constants — all null results so far. The truths are fixed.

**Physics requires it:** If constants drift, no measurement means anything. If the laws change, no experiment is replicable. The entire structure of experimental science depends on immutability. D = 0 in the mathematical channel. Zero drift. Ever. That's not a convenience — it's the reason physics works.

### 4. Simple

**God:** Not composed of parts. Divine simplicity — His attributes are not separable components bolted together. They are aspects of one undivided reality.

**Math:** Built from irreducible axioms. The Kolmogorov complexity of mathematics is minimal — maximal output from minimal input. The simplest description that generates all the outputs IS the most fundamental.

**Physics requires it:** Occam's razor isn't aesthetic. It's Kolmogorov complexity applied. Physics actively selects for the simplest theory that accounts for the data — not because physicists like elegance, but because a universe that runs on irreducible axioms is compressible. One that doesn't isn't describable. A non-simple ground would require infinite information to specify, which means no finite mind could ever grasp the laws — and yet finite minds DO grasp them. Simplicity is structural.

---

### 5. Consistent

**God:** Cannot contradict Himself. Internal coherence is not a preference — it's His nature.

**Math:** A ∧ ¬A destroys the system. One contradiction in a mathematical framework and everything follows (ex falso quodlibet) — which means nothing follows. Consistency is not optional. It's the thing that makes math mean anything at all.

**Physics requires it:** One contradiction and the whole system explodes. Paraconsistent physics is a toy, not a working framework. Every physical prediction depends on logical consistency. Remove it and the word "prediction" loses its meaning.

### 6. Universal

**God:** Omnipresent in authority. Not "very large" — His rule extends to every point, every domain, every scale.

**Math:** Same everywhere. No culture where 2 + 2 ≠ 4. No galaxy where the Pythagorean theorem fails. No scale where logic stops working. Mathematical truth is not local.

**Physics requires it:** If laws aren't the same everywhere, no experiment is repeatable. A measurement in Geneva means nothing for a measurement in Tokyo. Physics stops. The universality of physical law depends absolutely on the universality of mathematical truth.

### 7. Immaterial

**God:** Spirit. Not made of matter. Not detectable by physical instruments directly — known by effects.

**Math:** Non-physical. Numbers have no mass. Equations have no location. The Pythagorean theorem weighs nothing. Mathematical objects are entirely immaterial — known only by their effects on the physical world and by the minds that comprehend them.

**Physics requires it:** The most powerful forces governing the physical universe are themselves non-physical. F = ma has no mass. The laws of thermodynamics are not made of heat. A materialist can say "math is patterns in brains" — but the patterns are ABOUT something that has no physical substrate. The map is physical. The territory isn't. And physics runs on the territory, not the map.

### 8. Foundational

**God:** Ground of being. Everything that exists depends on Him. He depends on nothing.

**Math:** Underlies all reasoning. Physics runs on math. Chemistry runs on physics. Biology runs on chemistry. Pull math and everything above it collapses. There is no layer beneath mathematics except the ground itself.

**Physics requires it:** This is tautological. Physics cannot compute without mathematics. Every equation, every prediction, every measurement, every model. Pull the math and physics isn't a field — it's a collection of impressions with no structure.

---

### 9. Self-Existent

**God:** A se — from Himself. Not caused. Not dependent. Not derived from anything prior.

**Math:** Not dependent on anything else. We don't invent mathematics — we discover it. The truths were there before we arrived. They don't depend on human minds, physical reality, or any other contingent thing for their existence.

**Physics requires it:** If math depends on something else, what? And does THAT thing have the same 24 properties? You're back to the same entity under a different name. The chain of dependency either regresses infinitely or terminates at something self-existent. Mathematics behaves as if it terminates. The unreasonable effectiveness of math in physics is powerful evidence — math works because it was there first.

### 10. Infinite

**God:** Without limit. Not "very large" — unbounded in every attribute.

**Math:** Unbounded structures. No largest prime. No final decimal of π. The natural numbers never end. Cantor showed there are multiple sizes of infinity — infinity itself has structure. Math is the only human language that can speak meaningfully about the infinite.

**Physics requires it:** Gödel. Any consistent system rich enough to contain arithmetic generates truths it can't prove from within. The structure is inherently unbounded — not as a preference, but as a proven theorem. And if God is infinite and math is the only language that handles infinity, math is the only adequate language for the ground of physics. Every other language hits a wall at the finite.

### 11. Perfect

**God:** No error. Not "very accurate" — without defect.

**Math:** The truth itself does not err. A theorem, once proved, is proved forever. It doesn't approximate — it is exact. The territory is perfect; our maps are the approximations.

**Physics requires it:** A law with an error isn't a law. It's an approximation. Physics doesn't run on approximations at the ground level — it runs on exact mathematical structure that we approximate with measurements. The perfection is in the territory, not the map. If the territory itself could err, the concept of "measurement error" would have no meaning — error relative to what?

### 12. True

**God:** "I am the Truth" (John 14:6). Not "tells the truth" — IS the truth. The ground against which all other claims are measured.

**Math:** Truth-bearing by definition. Mathematical truths are the paradigm case of truth. When philosophers need an example of something indisputably true, they reach for 2 + 2 = 4. Math doesn't just contain truths — it IS the structure of truth.

**Physics requires it:** If math could lie — if it could give you outputs that don't follow from inputs — the entire enterprise collapses. Every physical prediction is a mathematical derivation. If the derivation can't be trusted, the prediction can't be trusted. If truth isn't structural, science isn't possible.

### 13. Rational

**God:** The Logos. John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Logos." Divine reason. Divine word. The rational principle underlying all things.

**Math:** Governed by logic. Mathematics IS rational thought in its purest form. No contradictions permitted. No illogic tolerated. Every step follows from the previous by necessity.

**Physics requires it:** If math weren't internally logical, derivation wouldn't work. Every proof chain, every calculation, every prediction requires that each step follows rationally from the last. Remove rationality and math becomes noise. Math becomes noise and physics becomes guessing.

### 14. Order-Giving

**God:** Orders creation. "And God said, let there be..." (Genesis 1). Not passive — actively structuring. The source of form itself.

**Math:** Structures reality. Gives form to physics. Without mathematical structure, physics has no equations, no symmetries, no conservation laws, no predictions. Math doesn't just describe order — it GIVES order. The structure of reality is mathematical structure.

**Physics requires it:** Structure requires something that imposes structure. No ordering principle, no physics. The universe doesn't just happen to be ordered — it is ordered BY something. That something has mathematical character. Remove the order-giving property and the universe is structureless chaos.

### 15. Law-Giving

**God:** Defines constraints. The Decalogue. "You shall" and "you shall not." Boundaries are not limitations — they are the conditions for flourishing.

**Math:** Defines constraints. Dictates what is possible and what is forbidden. F = ma is a constraint. Conservation laws are constraints. The laws of thermodynamics are constraints. Math doesn't just permit — it forbids.

**Physics requires it:** Constraints are what physics IS. Every equation is a constraint on what can happen. Remove law-giving and you have no equations, no predictions, no science. A universe without mathematical constraints has no physics — just events with no pattern.

### 16. Intelligible

**God:** Reveals Himself as knowable. Not hidden by necessity — knowable by design. "His invisible attributes have been clearly seen, being understood through what has been made" (Romans 1:20).

**Math:** Knowable through reason. Invites investigation. Mathematical truths don't hide — they reveal themselves to minds that seek them. The entire history of mathematics is unveiling.

**Physics requires it:** Wigner's "unreasonable effectiveness." If the universe weren't intelligible, math wouldn't apply to it. Intelligibility isn't our ability to understand — it's the universe's property of BEING understandable. A universe that is law-governed but unintelligible isn't one we could do science in. The intelligibility is a structural feature of reality, not an accident of human cognition.

### 17. Necessary for Knowledge

**God:** "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge" (Proverbs 1:7). Not just "associated with" knowledge — the precondition for it.

**Math:** Required for understanding anything quantitative, structural, or relational. No science without math. No engineering without math. No physics, no chemistry, no quantitative reasoning of any kind.

**Physics requires it:** You literally cannot do physics without math. Try. Write one physical law without mathematics. Make one quantitative prediction without a number. Measure one thing without a unit. Math isn't a tool physics uses — it's the language physics thinks in. Remove it and physics doesn't become primitive. It becomes impossible.

**The kill shot:** Try to argue against this. You'll use logic (math) to structure your argument, numbers (math) to cite evidence, and rational inference (math) to draw conclusions. The denial uses the thing being denied. Self-defeating.

### 18. Invariant

**God:** Same across all contexts. Not one God in Israel and a different God in Rome. Same nature, same character, same attributes — everywhere, always, to everyone.

**Math:** Same across contexts. Frame-independent. 2 + 2 = 4 in every reference frame, every coordinate system, every language, every century. The truths don't transform when you change perspective. They are the things that DON'T change.

**Physics requires it:** Lorentz invariance. General covariance. The deepest principles of modern physics are invariance principles — the laws must be the same in every reference frame. This is not aesthetic preference. It's structural. Einstein built relativity on it. Remove invariance and you cannot write physics that works for more than one observer.

### 19. Non-Local

**God:** Not confined to space. Not "in" a location the way objects are in locations. Present everywhere without being a spatial entity.

**Math:** Not confined to space. The number 7 is not located anywhere. The Pythagorean theorem doesn't have an address. Mathematical truths are not spatial entities — they have no location because location is a property of physical objects and math isn't one.

**Physics requires it:** The laws aren't located anywhere. Gravity doesn't have an address. F = ma is not "in" a place. If the laws were local — if they existed AT a location rather than governing FROM no location — they'd be physical objects subject to other laws, and you'd need meta-laws to govern the law-objects, and those meta-laws would need to be non-local anyway. Non-locality of the laws is not optional.

### 20. Transcendent

**God:** Above and beyond creation. Not reducible to creation. Not a creature. The maker, not the made.

**Math:** Not reducible to physical systems. Mathematical truths would hold even if the universe didn't exist. The truth of 2 + 2 = 4 doesn't depend on there being physical things to count. Math transcends physics.

**Physics requires it:** If the laws aren't above the physical systems they govern, they're just descriptions of behavior, not explanations. The law of gravity isn't made of gravity. The equation isn't made of the thing it describes. That irreducibility IS transcendence. And physics needs its laws to be explanatory, not merely descriptive — otherwise "why" questions have no answers and physics is stamp-collecting.

### 21. Objective

**God:** Independent of opinion. Not "true for me" — true. Period. His nature doesn't change based on who's observing.

**Math:** Independent of opinion. 2 + 2 = 4 regardless of what anyone thinks about it. Mathematical truths are not social constructions, not cultural products, not matters of perspective. They are objective.

**Physics requires it:** If the laws aren't independent of opinion, they're not laws. That's not philosophy — that's what "law" means. A law that changes based on who's looking at it is not a law. It's a preference. Physics built on preferences isn't physics. It's sociology.

### 22. Unified

**God:** Internally coherent whole. Not a committee. Not an assembly of independent parts. One God, one nature, one coherent reality.

**Math:** Internally coherent whole. Seemingly unrelated branches turn out to be deeply connected. Number theory links to geometry (algebraic geometry). Algebra links to topology (algebraic topology). The Langlands program suggests all major branches are aspects of one underlying structure.

**Physics requires it:** The whole project of unification — electroweak, GUTs, quantum gravity — assumes the laws form a coherent whole. If they didn't, there'd be no reason to expect them to be reconcilable. The unity of physics isn't a hope — it's a structural requirement for consistency across domains. A disunified math would produce a disunified physics where different domains obey different logics. That's not a universe. It's a contradiction.

### 23. Generative

**God:** Produces structures from Himself. Creates from nothing. From one Word, everything.

**Math:** Produces structures from finite axioms. The natural numbers generate the integers, which generate the rationals, which generate the reals, which generate the complex numbers, which generate the algebraic structures that govern quantum field theory. From almost nothing — everything.

**Physics requires it:** A few equations generate all of classical mechanics. A handful of physical constants and mathematical laws generate the entire observable universe. Physics runs on the generative property — minimal input, maximal output. If math weren't generative, you'd need a separate equation for every physical event. Infinite information to describe anything. Science would be impossible.

### 24. Judging

**God:** Distinguishes righteous from unrighteous. The judgment layer — input, standard, evaluation, verdict, outcome. The operation that separates what aligns from what doesn't.

**Math:** Distinguishes true from false. Every mathematical operation is a judgment — this follows, that doesn't. This is valid, that isn't. This is proved, that isn't. Math doesn't tolerate ambiguity at the ground level. True or false. Valid or invalid. The evaluation is absolute.

**Physics requires it:** Remove the discrimination between true and false and you can't do science at all. Every experiment is a judgment: does the data match the prediction? Every theory is a judgment: does the math work? Every measurement is a judgment: is this within the error bars? Science IS judgment. Physics IS evaluation against a standard. Remove the judging property and science becomes noise.

## The Kolmogorov Argument

Twenty-four structural properties shared across three domains.

**Option A:** One entity — God — authors mathematics with His own properties, and physics runs on that mathematics. One explanation. Twenty-four properties accounted for. Compression ratio: 24:1.

**Option B:** Coincidence. Mathematics just happens to share 24 properties with the God of classical theism. Physics just happens to require all 24. The correspondence is accidental. Each property needs its own separate explanation for why it appears in all three domains.

Kolmogorov complexity is the measure of the shortest description that generates a given output. Option A is the shortest description. Option B requires 24 independent explanations, each of which must also explain why it happens to match the other 23.

The compression ratio is not 2:1 or 3:1. It's 24:1. And it's tridirectional, which means the alternative doesn't just need to explain 24 shared properties between two domains — it needs to explain why all 24 propagate coherently through three domains in one direction.

This is not an argument from analogy. Analogies share a few surface features. This is 24 structural properties, every one of which physics dies without, every one of which is a classical divine attribute, all pointing the same direction.

At what number does coincidence become irrational?
