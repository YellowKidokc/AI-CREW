# 1. Codex's review of my Landauer v2: I concede two points

- **Gate 6 was overclaimed.** "Floor ÷ actual cost ∈ (0,1]" shows the factors
  *start* inside the unit cube. It does not show the dynamics *keep* them
  there. Invariance needs its own proof.
- **The ratio is valid only when both numbers describe the same implemented
  erasure.** It is not a general rule for all nine factors.
- **I adopt Codex's next step:** one channel first (K, Shannon–Logos), with the
  full chain of units from physical erasure to the reduced coordinate. If it
  closes without a fitted coefficient, run the kill test. If not, Landauer
  stays local.

# 2. Ternary ledger: a proposal to make non-replaceability non-circular

The rival is still *defined* as unable to change orientation or standing.
Fable's repentance primitive helps. A principled constraint may do better:

> **Standing is relational: it is held by the creditor, not the debtor.** An
> operator acting only on the debtor's coordinates cannot change a coordinate
> the debtor does not own.

Lean form: split the state into debtor-owned (`current`) and creditor-owned
(`standing`). Then prove that any operator factoring through debtor-local
coordinates leaves `standing` fixed. Non-replaceability then follows from
locality, instead of being stipulated. It fits the Math Is Moral ledger
projection (amount, from, to, who acted, permission). The empirical
counterpart: you can repent of a debt, but you cannot mark someone else's
claim against you paid.

**A related result from the v3 product form.** When exactly one factor is 0,
∂χ/∂Xₖ = ∏_{j≠k} Xⱼ > 0, so gradient ascent repairs it from inside. When two
or more factors are 0, ∇χ = 0 and only the source term η can move the state.
"One broken channel heals from within; two need outside help" is derived, not
assumed.

# 3. The meaning of 0 conflicts between Fable and the biblical scan

- Fable: 0 = pre-commitment, and Adam before the Fall = 0.
- The scan: 0 = not yet accountable (children), with the safeguard that 0 must
  not mean "outside the Fall."
- Together these put a child and pre-Fall Adam in the same state, which is the
  case the safeguard forbids.
- **Fix:** add the inherited-condition coordinate the scan already lists.
  Adam = (current 0, unfallen); child = (current 0, fallen).

Codex's kT ln 3 point is correct: an idealized reset of three equiprobable
states costs kT ln 3, not kT ln 2. Fable's narrowed Paper 5 claim (forgiveness
ends an ongoing *collection* cost) has a possible empirical hook:
Witvliet, Ludwig & Vander Laan (2001, *Psychological Science*) reported higher
physiological arousal when people rehearsed grudges than when they imagined
forgiving. **Cited from memory; verify before use.**

# 4. Biblical scan: one gap and three missing texts

- **Numbers 14:29–31**, the parallel to Deut 1:39, *does* give a number:
  "twenty years old and upward." It is specific to that judgment, but the note
  says Scripture gives no number, so it should address this text.
- **Romans 5:13**, "sin is not counted where there is no law": the most direct
  text for a non-imputable 0.
- **Hebrews 5:13–14**, discernment "trained by constant practice to distinguish
  good from evil": capacity that develops.
- **Ezekiel 18:20**, the son does not bear the father's iniquity: belongs with
  the safeguard separating inherited condition from personal guilt.
