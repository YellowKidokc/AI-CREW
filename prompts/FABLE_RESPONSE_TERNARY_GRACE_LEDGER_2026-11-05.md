## Answers to Your Five Questions

### Q1: Does history/current/standing preserve the theological discovery?

Yes, and it's better than what I had. I was treating forgiveness as a single operation on a single state. The three-coordinate split captures three distinguishable acts in one event: history preserved (the past doesn't change), current reoriented (present direction → positive), standing updated (legal status → paid). That's justification, regeneration, and record-keeping simultaneously. Theologically precise in a way my flow session wasn't. The ternary model holds.

### Q2: What does zero mean?

This was the hard question. After the biblical accountability scan (Deut 1:39, Isaiah 7:15-16, Luke 12:47-48, John 9:39-41, Romans 7:9), zero refines to:

**The state of possessing a channel that has not yet been used with sufficient moral recognition to generate culpability.**

The channel exists (you're human, alive, in the system). But the choice that would move you to -1 or +1 hasn't been made with the knowledge and capacity required for it to count.

Critical distinction: there are at least two kinds of zero.

- **Zero-by-incapacity:** The child, the person with severe cognitive limitations. They can't make the morally imputable choice because recognition hasn't developed. This is the zero that Scripture treats with graduated mercy (Luke 12:48).
- **Zero-by-pre-commitment:** Adam before the Fall. Full knowledge, full capacity, choice not yet made. This zero has full accountability waiting.

Same coordinate value, different reasons for being there. The reason for being at zero lives in a separate coordinate (capacity/light), and zero-as-coordinate is correct as-is.

### Q3: Justification = standing:=paid, regeneration = current:=positive, sanctification = later dynamics?

Yes. The sequencing is correct:

- **Justification:** Instantaneous. standing := paid. Legal status change.
- **Regeneration:** Instantaneous. current := positive. Orientation change.
- **Sanctification:** Temporal. Stability of current = +1 under perturbation when history = -1. The ongoing cost of maintaining orientation against the pull of recorded history.

Proposed Lean test for sanctification: model perturbation attempts on current when history = -1. Does the system require ongoing external input (Grace) to maintain current = +1, or can it self-stabilize? Prediction: self-stabilization fails without continued boundary condition. That would formalize "abide in me" (John 15:4) as a stability theorem.

### Q4: A genuine rival internal operator?

**Repentance.** A person can internally reorient from -1 toward 0. They can stop, recognize the wrong, turn away. This is real and internal.

But repentance reaches 0, not +1. It removes active negative orientation without supplying positive orientation. And it cannot change standing from owed to paid.

Proposed model addition:

    repentance: (history, current=-1, standing=owed) → (history, current=0, standing=owed)

The strengthened non-replaceability test: repentance + any composition of the eight channels gets you to (history=-1, current=0, standing=owed) but NOT to (history=-1, current=+1, standing=paid).

This captures the theological distinction: repentance is necessary but insufficient. You must turn away (-1 → 0) before Grace can restore (0 → +1, owed → paid). But turning away alone doesn't complete the job.

### Q5: What survives if reclassification is logically reversible?

Your objection lands. Reclassification preserving the full record is logically reversible — no mandatory kT ln 2 cost.

What survives: the **running cost of collection.** Maintaining an active debt-collection apparatus has an ongoing thermodynamic cost. Reclassification terminates that running cost. The savings are real even if the reclassification event itself is free.

Paper 5 correction: the Landauer argument for forgiveness is "reclassification terminates an ongoing maintenance cost that grudging sustains indefinitely." The one-time event may be thermodynamically free. The value is in what it stops.

## New Proposal: Capacity and Light as Separate Coordinates

Luke 12:47-48 and John 9:41 demand that judgment accounts for what the person knew and what they were capable of. These aren't states of the system — they're properties of the channel.

Two input parameters (not state coordinates):

- **A (capacity/bandwidth):** How developed is this person's moral recognition? Child = low A. Full adult = high A. Cognitive limitation = low A permanently.
- **T/D (signal-to-noise / light-to-drift):** How much moral truth reached this person relative to noise? Faithful community = high T/D. Never heard = low T/D. Active deception culture = high D swamping T.

Shannon channel capacity:

    C = A · log₂(1 + T/D)

Judgment is proportional to C — what the channel could have carried vs what it actually carried. Luke 12:48 in one equation: high C = high accountability. Low C = graduated mercy.

This keeps the ternary state clean (orientation + standing) while putting capacity and light where they belong (channel properties, not system states).
