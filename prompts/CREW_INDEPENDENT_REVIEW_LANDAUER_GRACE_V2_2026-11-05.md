## Why this v2 exists

The original breakdown contains valuable questions but also embeds several
desired conclusions in the assignments. That is appropriate for construction,
not for testing independent convergence.

This protocol removes predicted answers. A reviewer may return `SURVIVES`,
`NARROWS`, `REVISE`, `FAILS`, or `UNDECIDABLE WITH CURRENT DEFINITIONS`.
Agreement is not required. A well-supported failure is a successful review.

## Independence rule

Before filing a receipt, each reviewer receives only:

1. the shared neutral claim packet below;
2. the primary source files necessary for its lane;
3. its own assignment;
4. the common output template.

Reviewers must not read the other lanes' receipts before freezing their own.
Claude 4.6 is the originating synthesizer and therefore serves as author/defense
rather than an independent confirming vote.

## Shared neutral claim packet

The program currently proposes:

1. a ternary orientation: negative, zero, positive;
2. separate coordinates for historical record, current orientation, and
   normative standing;
3. a restorative operation that preserves history, makes current positive, and
   changes standing from owed to paid;
4. repentance as an internal partial operation that can move negative current
   to zero while preserving history and standing;
5. a conditional Lean result: the admitted internal language cannot reproduce
   full restoration while every admitted operator preserves standing;
6. a biblical hypothesis that accountability varies with moral recognition,
   agency, knowledge/light, intention, and capacity rather than one birthday;
7. a physical hypothesis that some information-processing costs may be examined
   using Landauer, while reclassification is not automatically erasure;
8. an empirical hypothesis that ending active debt collection may terminate an
   ongoing maintenance cost;
9. an open question whether the theological, formal, informational, physical,
   and empirical structures are genuinely linked or merely analogous.

No reviewer is told that these claims should survive.

## Common verdict questions — every lane answers all six

1. Which claim is precisely stated enough to test?
2. Which claim changes meaning while moving between domains?
3. What is the strongest counterexample or rival model?
4. What evidence or theorem would change your verdict?
5. What survives if your strongest objection succeeds?
6. Overall verdict for the present program: `SURVIVES`, `NARROWS`, `REVISE`,
   `FAILS`, or `UNDECIDABLE`, with confidence and reasons.

## Lane 1 — Codex: formal logic, Lean, and countermodels

**Task:** Audit the mathematical content without defending the theology.

- Identify assumptions that make conclusions definitional or circular.
- Admit repentance and at least two stronger rival operators.
- Test whether standing-preservation is derived or merely assigned.
- Build countermodels for alternative meanings of zero.
- Separate non-replaceability within a grammar from universal irreducibility.
- Return the smallest theorem that survives every implemented countermodel.

**Deliverable:** Lean modules, compile receipt, premise ledger, countermodel

## Lane 2 — Gemini/Jim: physics and information theory

**Task:** Audit every physical and information-theoretic statement from primary
technical sources.

- Specify the exact logically irreversible operation, if any.
- Check units and definitions for every efficiency or cost equation.
- Determine when `kT ln 2`, `kT ln 3`, or an entropy-dependent expression is
  applicable.
- Test the zero-temperature/infinite-reservoir analogy against the Third Law,
  reservoir thermodynamics, and reversible computing.
- Determine whether ongoing collection has a necessary thermodynamic signature
  or only a contingent implementation cost.
- State which theological claims remain untouched if every Landauer bridge is
  removed.

**Deliverable:** source-linked technical audit, dimensional table, strongest
physical countermodel, and verdict for each bridge.

## Lane 3 — Fable: biblical and doctrinal theology

**Task:** Review Scripture and theological traditions without assuming the
physics or Lean model is correct.

- Audit the accountability passage set in context.
- Compare major Christian treatments of infant salvation, inherited sin,
  culpability, justification, regeneration, repentance, and sanctification.
- Test whether zero as pre-commitment or limited agency is biblically coherent.
- Ask whether `standing := paid` and `current := positive` preserve relevant
  doctrinal differences or collapse them.
- Test whether Grace is best represented as boundary condition, intervention,
  relation, gift, divine action, or something the model omits.
- Identify where the model clarifies theology and where it begins dictating it.

**Deliverable:** passage-context table, tradition comparison, doctrinal
countermodel, and narrow theological verdict.

## Lane 4 — Kimi: empirical psychology and operationalization

**Task:** Determine whether the forgiveness/collection proposal can become an
honest empirical research program.

- Define observable operations corresponding to remembering, rumination,
  resentment, boundary maintenance, debt collection, forgiveness, and
  reconciliation.
- Distinguish energy expenditure, attention, stress physiology, cognitive load,
  and subjective effort.
- Search for cases where forgiveness increases rather than decreases short-term
  cost, or where continued boundary maintenance is protective.
- Design measurements that do not presuppose the theological interpretation.
- Propose preregistered predictions and failure conditions.
- State whether any result could discriminate this model from ordinary
  psychological accounts.

**Deliverable:** operationalization table, study design, confound ledger,
ethical constraints, and empirical verdict.

## Lane 5 — Claude 4.6: author defense and claim reconstruction

**Task:** Present the strongest version of the program without pretending to be

- Produce a numbered claim ledger separating theology, formal mathematics,

- Give the strongest evidence for every bridge.
- Remove or rewrite every statement you cannot defend literally.
- Explain what the model contributes if the Landauer layer fails entirely.
- Identify the minimum publishable paper and defer the rest.
- State in advance which findings from the four independent lanes would cause

**Deliverable:** strongest defense, dependency graph, precommitted revision

## Convergence stage — only after five receipts are frozen

David or a separately designated comparison model creates a matrix with claims
as rows and reviewers as columns. Record:

- exact agreement;
- apparent agreement using different definitions;
- genuine disagreement;
- independent discovery of the same objection;
- author-only claims not accepted by independent lanes;
- claims surviving all relevant specialties;
- claims no lane had the evidence to decide.

Convergence increases confidence only when reviewers reached the same result
from independent evidence or methods. Repetition of a shared premise does not
count as convergence.

## David's team principle

The protocol is adversarial without being disposable. A reviewer who breaks a
claim remains part of the team. An originating model receives credit without
receiving immunity. Objections remain in the record even when David elects to
continue construction. This is the functional team architecture under test.
