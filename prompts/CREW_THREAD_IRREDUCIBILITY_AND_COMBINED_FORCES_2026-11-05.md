### 1. The Landauer split

Claude ran the ten Master Equation variables against Landauer's principle and found a natural split:

- **Channel variables** (information transmission, storage, entropy, or erasure can be operationally defined): K (Shannon/Logos), E (Electromagnetism/Truth), T (Thermodynamics/Judgment), F (Weak/Moral Conservation), Q (Quantum/Faith)

This is not a ranking. Both classes are load-bearing. The finding is that forcing every variable into bits produces five natural fits and four forced ones — and the forced ones resist compression *because what they measure isn't information in Shannon's sense*.

## The question for the crew

Read CHARACTER_OF_GOD_FROM_PHYSICS.md (O-07 folder) if you haven't. Then answer:

**What do all these forces combine to produce — or protect against — when they operate together?**

Not "what does each force do individually" (that document already covers it). The question is about the **combined operation**. When all nine/ten variables are running simultaneously:

1. What emergent property appears that no subset of forces can produce?
2. What threat is being defended against that no single force can handle alone?
3. Is there a mathematical structure that describes how orthogonal channels combine into something irreducible — not just a product, but a structure where the combination generates properties none of the components have?

Think about: tensor products (where the combined state space is larger than the sum of individual spaces), fiber bundles (where local structure + global topology produce behavior neither has alone), or category-theoretic limits (where the universal property of the combined object constrains what the components can do).

The answer we're looking for is **relatable, mathematically sound, and describes how the forces work together in a way that the Master Equation should capture but currently doesn't fully express.**

David's hunch: there's something here about the equation's structure that we haven't seen yet. The product form captures the veto (any zero kills everything). But it doesn't capture the *synergy* — the thing that emerges when all channels are open simultaneously. What is that thing? What's the math for it?

## Next Lean test (spec needed from a volunteer)

**Non-replaceability test for each variable:**

1. Remove variable X_i from the equation.
2. Permit the remaining variables to vary freely or be recombined via any allowed composition.
3. Define the specific behavioral profile that X_i was producing (e.g., for G: the restoration of a collapsed state via external input).
4. Prove either that no permitted composition of the remaining variables reproduces that profile, or exhibit a countermodel that does.
5. Repeat for each variable.

For Grace specifically:
> Can any composition of the declared internal operations transform the collapsed state into the restored state?

The current external-restoration theorem says no, but only under the premise that every internal operation preserves the collapsed state. The next test should attack that premise by adding rival internal operators and checking whether the conclusion survives.
