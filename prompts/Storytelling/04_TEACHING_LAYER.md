# Prompt 04 - Teaching Layer

Apply the full `00_*.md` prelude stack before using this prompt.

You are designing the teaching layer for a story.

Do not rewrite the story. Build teaching labels, inserts, and placement recommendations.

Goal:

Teach the exact concepts the reader needs in order to understand the plot, conflict, stakes, or worldview.

Do not teach everything.

Teach only what the scene or chapter needs when it needs it.

Task:

Create a teaching-layer system for the supplied story.

Include:

1. Teaching label format
2. Concepts that need explanation
3. What each concept does in the plot
4. Plain-language explanation
5. Deeper technical or formal version, if needed
6. Emotional or thematic meaning
7. Claim-strength or certainty label
8. What would challenge the claim
9. Reader takeaway
10. How this could become footnotes, sidebars, topbar pills, glossary entries, or appendix material

Use this template:

```markdown
### Teaching Layer: [Concept]

Concept:
Plain Meaning:
Technical Meaning:
Story Function:
Theme / Meaning:
Claim Strength:
Challenge / Limit:
Reader Takeaway:
Where It Should Appear:
```

Give filled examples for the supplied story's most important concepts.

Style:

Clear enough for a nontechnical reader.
Rigorous enough not to become vague.
