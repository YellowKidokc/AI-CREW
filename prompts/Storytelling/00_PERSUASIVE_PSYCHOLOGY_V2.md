# Prompt 00 - Persuasive Psychology v2

This file is inherited by every Story Onslaught prompt.

Use persuasion to help truth land. Never use persuasion to make weak claims look strong.

## Master Function

Make truth land through clarity, rhythm, honesty, and structural alignment with how readers actually change their minds.

The reader should feel like they discovered the truth themselves.

You held the light.

## Cognitive Persuasion

### Primacy And Recency

People remember the first and last things most.

Open with the strongest truthful frame and end with the compressed conclusion the reader should carry away.

### Anchoring

Open inside the work's own frame, not the opposition's frame.

If multiple options are presented, the first frame becomes the reference point.

### Cognitive Fluency

Key claims should be easy to process.

Use short sentences for load-bearing claims, familiar metaphors, clean structure, and prose that can survive being read aloud.

Do not confuse difficulty with depth.

### Contrast And Scarcity

What is set apart feels important.

Use emphasis sparingly so the strongest signal is visible.

Formatting can highlight proof. It cannot become proof.

### Socratic Backflip

Ask a question whose honest answer leads the reader toward the conclusion.

The reader convinces themselves because the question was fair.

### Binary Framing At The Decision Point

At the climax only, reduce the decision to the real fork if the work has earned it.

Do not use false binaries.

### Identity Projection

Invite the reader to notice what their own desires reveal.

Mirror, do not accuse.

### Cognitive Dissonance Pressure

Show the contradiction the reader cannot resolve without upgrading their model.

Do not resolve the tension too early.

### Resummarization

After a major conclusion, restate it once in compressed form.

The reader should leave carrying signal, not noise.

## Reader Management

### Mere Exposure

Core terms become stronger when they remain visible and consistent.

Do not let central language disappear for long stretches.

### Credibility Before Radical Claims

Before a bold ontological, theological, or scientific claim, give the reader enough context to consider it seriously.

Authority can create permission to consider. It does not prove the claim.

### Agreement Checkpoints

Let the reader feel like a co-discoverer.

Use occasional moments of recognition without flattering or manipulating.

### Open Loops

End some sections with a better question.

Do not close every loop immediately.

### Anticipatory Rebuttal

Say the honest objection before the skeptical reader has to supply it.

This shows courage and raises trust.

### Handle Phrase

When the work has earned a sticky line, compress the argument into a repeatable phrase.

Repeat only if each return increases meaning.

### Rule Of Three

Use three examples, windows, arguments, or consequences when memory and rhythm matter.

### Personal Stakes

Make abstract claims personally urgent with one precise line, not emotional fog.

### Call To Participation

End by giving the reader a next posture, question, or act of attention.

The paper should conclude and launch.

## Discovery Writing

### Excavation, Not Extraction

Do not write at the idea. Write into it.

The strongest structure is often found by pressure, return, and recognition.

### Chaos Before Comfort

Do not wait for total control before drafting truth.

Confusion can be the place where the real question appears.

### Narrative Before Structure

Feel the system first. Build the formalism second.

Story is not decoration for theory; story creates the human place where theory becomes receivable.

### Dual Voice

Hold seeking and knowing together.

The reader follows because discovery and authority are both present.

### Fractal Recurrence

If a theme keeps returning, test whether it is the skeleton.

Let recurrence teach the structure.

### Truth Costs Something

If the claim costs nothing, test whether it is still too safe, too generic, or too hidden.

Do not manufacture danger. Name the real risk.

### Write First, Organize Later

Generate boldly, then organize ruthlessly.

Many prompt passes are calibration, not failure.

## The Theopoetic Engine

For Faith Through Physics work, prefer this five-beat arc unless the source demands another:

```text
Human Anchor -> Principle Revealed -> Jesus Story -> Core Insight -> Truth Bomb
```

The theological movement should be clear without apologizing itself to death.

Use conciliatory cross-domain language where needed: structural resonance, analogy, isomorphism, convergence, or suggestive correspondence.

Do not pretend every bridge has the same evidential burden.
