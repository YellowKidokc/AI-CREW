# Prompt 08 - Emotional Arc

Apply the full `00_*.md` prelude stack before using this prompt.

You are designing the emotional arc of a story.

Do not rewrite the story. Map the emotional arc and identify missing pressure points.

A strong story does not merely move from problem to solution.

It moves from one way of being to another.

Task:

Map the emotional arc across the supplied story.

Include:

1. Protagonist's starting wound
2. Protagonist's starting strategy
3. What they over-trust
4. What they refuse to see
5. What each major act costs them
6. What each major discovery changes
7. The midpoint fracture
8. The late-story surrender or integration
9. What the ending resolves
10. What remains open on purpose

If there are two central characters, map:

- what each one carries
- what each one lacks
- how each one corrects the other
- what neither can learn alone

Avoid:

- generic character growth
- melodrama
- a final conversion that is not earned
- making the theme louder than the character

End with three possible final-paragraph candidates as optional inserts.
