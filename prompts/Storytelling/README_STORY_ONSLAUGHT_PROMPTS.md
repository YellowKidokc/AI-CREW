# Story Onslaught Prompt Pack

Purpose: generate multiple controlled versions of any story, then strip the best parts apart and reassemble a stronger draft.

These prompts are general. They can be used for fiction, nonfiction, teaching stories, memoir-shaped work, philosophical fiction, theology/science stories, founder stories, essays, or chapter rewrites.

Before every prompt, apply the full shared prelude stack:

```text
00_META_COGNITIVE_ARCHITECTURE.md
00_NARRATIVE_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md
00_OPENING_DEVICE_RECURRENCE_SYSTEM.md
00_PERSUASIVE_PSYCHOLOGY_V2.md
```

Those files are the shared operating system. They tell every specialist how to think: optimize for irreversible understanding, preserve epistemic integrity, track narrative pressure, compress symbols, manage cognitive load, simulate readers, preserve the opening promise, and make truth land without making weak claims look strong.

Use one prompt at a time. Do not ask one AI to do every angle at once.

Default rule: prompts 01-10 are **not rewrite prompts**. They diagnose, extract, map, label, and propose inserts. Prompt 11 may reassemble only after the gold has been harvested.

## Shared Mechanism

Every strong story has two curricula:

```text
Visible curriculum = what the plot appears to teach or solve.
Hidden curriculum = what the protagonist, author, or reader is actually learning underneath.
```

The best drafts make those two layers converge.

## Output Strategy

Prompts 01-10 create different controlled analyses, maps, section recommendations, teaching labels, and insert candidates. They should not rewrite the full source.

Prompt 11 harvests:

- best lines
- best structures
- best dialogue mechanics
- best emotional beats
- best teaching labels
- best audit boundaries
- best final load-bearing sentence

Then it rebuilds from fragments instead of averaging the drafts.
