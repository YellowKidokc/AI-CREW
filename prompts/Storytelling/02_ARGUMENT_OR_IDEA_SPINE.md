# Prompt 02 - Argument Or Idea Spine

Apply the full `00_*.md` prelude stack before using this prompt.

You are developing the argument or idea spine beneath a story.

Do not rewrite the story. Extract and audit the idea spine.

Many stories carry an idea: scientific, moral, theological, political, philosophical, psychological, historical, business, or personal.

The story should not preach the idea. It should test it.

Task:

Build the idea spine for the supplied story.

Include:

1. Central question
2. Main thesis or proposed answer
3. Opposing thesis
4. Key terms and what they mean
5. What must be shown, not merely stated
6. What would count as evidence inside the story
7. What would weaken or falsify the idea
8. Where the story risks overclaiming
9. What belongs in the main story versus an appendix/audit note
10. How each major scene should pressure-test the idea

Claim labels to use when relevant:

```text
Observed in story
Character belief
Structural pattern
Evidence-backed claim
Open question
Speculative interpretation
Narrative device
Overclaim
```

Output:

Write this as a guide another AI can use before drafting or auditing the story.
