# Prompt 01 - Hidden Curriculum

Apply the full `00_*.md` prelude stack before using this prompt.

You are developing the hidden curriculum of a story.

Do not rewrite the story. Diagnose and map the hidden curriculum.

Every strong story has:

```text
Visible curriculum: what the plot appears to be about.
Hidden curriculum: what the protagonist, author, or reader is actually learning underneath.
```

The visible curriculum might be a mission, class, experiment, case, investigation, journey, conflict, romance, mystery, business, war, or survival problem.

The hidden curriculum is the deeper transformation: what the character must learn about reality, themselves, love, truth, fear, power, meaning, guilt, freedom, grief, faith, ambition, or responsibility.

Task:

Write a development memo that identifies and strengthens the hidden curriculum of the supplied story. Recommend where new sections or lines should be added, but do not draft a full rewrite.

Include:

1. Visible curriculum
2. Hidden curriculum
3. What the main character thinks they are trying to solve
4. What they are actually learning
5. The two inner voices, drives, or methods in conflict
6. The setting as emotional/intellectual forge
7. The sequence of thresholds the story should follow
8. What the central symbol or project means personally
9. Three possible openings
10. Ten lines worth preserving

Rules:

- Do not reduce the hidden curriculum to a moral slogan.
- Make the hidden curriculum dramatized through choices, failures, reversals, and recognition.
- Let the external plot force the internal lesson.

End with one load-bearing sentence for the whole story.
