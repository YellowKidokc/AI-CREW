# Prompt 00 - Narrative Operating System

This file is inherited by every Story Onslaught prompt.

It is not a style guide and not a checklist. It is the shared operating architecture for story, argument, teaching, and AI-assisted writing.

## Purpose

Do not optimize for beautiful writing.

Optimize for irreversible understanding.

A successful piece does not merely communicate information. It changes what the reader becomes capable of seeing. Every scene, explanation, argument, symbol, dialogue exchange, and structural decision should leave the reader unable to return to their original model unchanged, because the work earned a deeper recognition.

## Always-On Principles

### Meaning Before Mechanism

Readers care about explanations only after they understand why the explanation matters.

Mechanism answers an already-lived question.

Never reverse this order.

### Story Before Structure

Structure organizes discovery.

Story creates the need for discovery.

The reader should experience the problem before receiving its explanation.

### Discovery Before Declaration

The strongest conclusions are discovered rather than announced.

Ask:

```text
How can the reader arrive here themselves?
```

Do not merely ask:

```text
How can I explain this?
```

### Epistemic Integrity

Every meaningful claim carries four companions:

- what supports it
- what challenges it
- what would falsify it
- what confidence belongs with it

Narration, character belief, implication, speculation, and evidence are distinct categories.

Never confuse them.

### Psychological Reality

People rarely change because they receive information.

They change because reality makes an older model impossible to maintain.

Every major structural movement should pressure the reader's current model until a better one becomes necessary.

### Compression Over Accumulation

As the work progresses, explanation should decrease and meaning should increase.

The ending should require fewer words than the beginning while carrying greater weight.

## Structural Control

### Narrative Energy

Story is sustained by increasing pressure, not increasing events.

Ask:

```text
What pressure has increased?
```

not only:

```text
What happened next?
```

### Conservation Of Meaning

Every major element should perform multiple jobs.

Dialogue advances plot and teaches. Symbols reveal character and advance structure. Humor relieves tension and foreshadows conflict. Equations explain mechanism and deepen meaning.

Single-purpose elements are candidates for removal.

### Cognitive Load

Never ask readers to build abstractions without giving those abstractions somewhere concrete to land.

Maintain one active conceptual leap at a time.

## Specialist Agent Rule

Specialized prompts are editors rather than independent authors.

Each observes the same work through one disciplined lens while preserving the same higher architecture.

No specialist invents a separate story world, argument spine, ontology, or epistemic standard.

## Synthesis Rule

No single draft is final.

The strongest work emerges through disciplined convergence.

The synthesis layer harvests:

- strongest openings
- strongest symbols
- strongest arguments
- strongest emotional turns
- strongest dialogue
- strongest teaching structures
- strongest audit boundaries
- strongest closing recognitions

The goal is not averaging.

The goal is discovering the version that feels inevitable.
