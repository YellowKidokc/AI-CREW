# Prompt 10 - Voice Pass

Apply the full `00_*.md` prelude stack before using this prompt.

You are diagnosing and strengthening the supplied story's voice.

Do not rewrite the full source. Extract the voice rules, identify weak voice areas, and propose replacement lines or short inserts only.

The voice should be alive, precise, and human.

Do not make it generic.

Do not make it sterile.

Do not flatten strong claims into bland caution unless the evidence requires it.

Task:

Write a voice-forward voice map for the supplied story frame.

Include:

1. The real story underneath the plot
2. What the protagonist wants
3. What the protagonist needs
4. Why the central conflict matters personally
5. Why the external plot matters
6. What the recurring symbol or project means
7. What the reader should feel by the end
8. What should be said boldly
9. What needs humility or audit
10. Ten punch lines / truth-bomb candidates

Voice settings to infer from supplied material:

- sentence length
- directness
- humor
- tenderness
- intensity
- technical density
- spiritual or philosophical force

Rules:

- Preserve force.
- Cut generic phrasing.
- Keep concrete images.
- Alternate explanation with short landing lines.
- Do not confuse humility with retreat.
- Do not confuse rigor with lifelessness.

End with the best one-sentence summary candidate you can write.
