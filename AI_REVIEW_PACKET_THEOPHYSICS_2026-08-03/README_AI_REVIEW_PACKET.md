# AI Review Packet: Theophysics / POF 2828

Prepared: 2026-08-03

## Purpose

This packet is for serious external review by AI systems or human reviewers.

The goal is not automatic approval.

The goal is genuine criticism:

- rate the claims;
- identify weak links;
- separate proof from bridge language;
- name overclaims;
- propose tests;
- state objections fairly;
- recommend what should be kept, downgraded, revised, cut, or left unchanged.

David may accept some objections, reject others, defer some, and intentionally leave some text unchanged. Review is evidence for revision, not a command system.

## Review Principles

1. Do not treat theological claims, scientific claims, Lean proofs, and analogies as the same kind of claim.
2. Do not say "physics proves the Trinity." The packet explicitly rejects that claim.
3. Identify when a statement is:
   - formally proved;
   - structurally modeled;
   - empirically suggested;
   - philosophically argued;
   - theologically asserted;
   - analogical;
   - speculative;
   - overclaimed.
4. Prefer precise objections over broad dismissal.
5. A reviewer should say what would change their rating.
6. A reviewer should identify the strongest part of the argument as well as the weakest part.

## Desired Output From Each Reviewer

Each reviewer should produce:

1. Overall rating.
2. Claim-by-claim objections.
3. Top five strongest claims.
4. Top five weakest or most dangerous claims.
5. Best hostile objection.
6. Suggested tests in Lean, Python/Colab, empirical research, or textual/theological analysis.
7. Recommended revision actions.
8. A list of claims that should not be changed despite reviewer discomfort, if the reviewer believes they are legitimately central and properly guarded.
