- Core Rule The load-bearing line of DG is: ```text L(n+1) = L(n) + C(n+1) ``` In words: A higher layer inherits the valid capabilities of the lower layer and adds one new irreducible capability class.

- DG rejects that because the higher layer must depend upon and preserve the lower.
