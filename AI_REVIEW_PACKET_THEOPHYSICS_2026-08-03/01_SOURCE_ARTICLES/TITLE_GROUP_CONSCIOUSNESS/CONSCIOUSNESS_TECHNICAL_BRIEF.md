# Consciousness Technical Brief

Status: internal technical canon pass, 2026-08-01  
Purpose: preserve the live structure while preventing cross-register overclaim.

## Executive Ruling

The consciousness stack has one public-safe center:

> Consciousness is being structurally modeled as self-referential, oriented relation.

The stack does not solve consciousness. It does not derive consciousness from scalar-field equilibrium. It does not prove qualia, souls, or human consciousness in Lean.

The scalar-field equilibrium chain is reduced to substrate analogy. The internal-regress argument remains live, but it has two named holes: the secular triad objection and Stage 4-5 agency wiring.

## Register Separation

### Theology

Allowed:

- Human persons are embodied souls, image-bearers, witnesses, moral agents, and receivers/responders to grace.
- Consciousness is read as a created capacity for relation to world, self, neighbor, and God.
- Christ functions as interpretive center for truth, moral cost, grace, and restoration.
- The knower/known/knowing-act structure can be interpreted theologically as a finite participation in a triadic ground.

Not allowed:

- Presenting theology as if neuroscience has proved it.
- Treating Christological language as a formal theorem.
- Saying Lean proves the soul.

### Philosophy

Allowed:

- Eliminativism faces a reflexive challenge because it must account for truth-assessing self-report without borrowing the witness it denies.
- Self-interpretation creates regress pressure.
- Infinite regress and brute terminus are inadequate explanations if the goal is self-grounding.
- A differentiated unity of knower, known, and knowing-act is a proposed adequate terminus.
- Godel, Tarski, and Turing may be cited as structural analogies about self-reference limits.

Not allowed:

- Treating formal-system theorems as direct proofs about consciousness.
- Claiming the triadic terminus is uniquely proven for philosophy of mind.

### Neuroscience / Science

Allowed:

- The hard problem remains contested.
- Neural correlates, cognitive models, animal behavior, attention, self-report, integrated information, and AI architectures are relevant empirical domains.
- The Consciousness Ladder can propose empirical targets, especially around animal evidence and machine coupling.
- Phi-threshold and collapse-test language are hypotheses or protocols only.

Not allowed:

- Claiming scalar-field equilibrium derives consciousness.
- Claiming Phi is validated as a Theophysics consciousness threshold.
- Claiming animal or AI consciousness is settled by the ladder.

### Theophysics Bridge

Allowed:

- Consciousness can be modeled as relation becoming self-referential and oriented.
- The internal-regress structure can be compared to triadic termination elsewhere in the canon.
- Coupling-gate logic can be used to test whether proposed triads preserve role profile, distinctness, dynamics, and invariant.
- The ladder can be used as a graded participation/coupling model.

Not allowed:

- Collapsing analogy into isomorphism.
- Presenting all five triadic instances as equally proven.
- Letting the weaker consciousness leg carry the same public force as Lean-verified Maxwell or Cross modules.

### Lean-Testable

Lean can test formal consequences after definitions are supplied.

Candidate Lean targets:

- If `Conscious x` is defined as `Rep x /\ SelfRef x /\ Oriented x`, prove each component is necessary.
- If `GroundingExit` has `InfiniteRegress`, `BruteTerminus`, and `SelfGroundingTerminus`, prove only the third satisfies `AdequateGrounding` under explicitly stated non-circular adequacy criteria.
- If a triad lacks role distinction, coupling invariant, or dynamic relation, prove it fails the gate.
- If a ladder rung has `O = 0`, prove no voluntary coupling follows under the model definitions.
- If deterministic self-evolution lacks an agency predicate, prove it cannot satisfy the defined agency/coupling condition.

Lean cannot test:

- Whether humans are conscious.
- Whether qualia exist.
- Whether God exists.
- Whether Christ is the metaphysical ground.
- Whether a physical animal or AI has O > 0.

## Internal-Regress Argument

Working form:

1. Conscious systems interpret.
2. A conscious system can interpret its own interpretive activity.
3. Self-interpretation generates a grounding question: what makes the interpretation trustworthy?
4. Infinite regress does not ground.
5. Brute terminus stops explanation.
6. A self-grounding terminus must preserve distinction without fragmentation and unity without collapse.
7. The proposed role profile is knower, known, and act of knowing.

Status: live structural-philosophical argument.

Open dependency: the move from "operator application" to "agency required" still needs repair. Deterministic self-evolution must be shown insufficient, not merely dismissed.
