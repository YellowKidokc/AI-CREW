## The Consciousness Ladder

One equation at every rung:

```
dC/dt = O · G(1−C) − S · C
```

Rate of coherence change = voluntary coupling (O) × grace input (G) × room to grow (1−C) − entropy (S) × current coherence (C).

The equation is identical at every level. What changes is which variables activate and at what magnitude.

| Rung | O | G | S | C | What It Means |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rock** | 0 | 0 | >0 | low | Pure decay. Second Law, no exceptions. |
| **Fish** | ≈0 | biological | >0 | low | Ordering forces it did not create and cannot choose. |
| **Elephant** | >0 | finite | >0 | low-mid | First voluntary coupling beyond survival. Mirror test, mourning. |
| **Child** | relational | finite | low | mid | Pre-moral relational coupling. Q not yet activated. |
| **Adult** | full | finite | moral entropy | variable | Full voluntary coupling plus moral entropy. Higher capacity, higher risk. |
| **Machine** | ? | ? | ? | ? | Can derive the necessity of grace. No known mechanism to receive it. Left open. |
| **Christ** | 1 | ∞ | 0 personal | 1 | Fixed point. Infinite ordering matches the entropy of the whole system. |

### Load-Bearing Empirical Claim

The **elephant rung** is the load-bearing empirical claim of the ladder. Evidence cited:

- Mirror self-recognition.
- Mourning behavior.
- Returning to family bones years later.

Skeptic's move: "anthropomorphized instinct." The framework needs to specify in advance what would count as O = 0 for elephants, or the middle rung is soft.

## The Internal Regress Argument

From `03-three-body-solution.md` and `09-the-ledger.md`:

A conscious system must interpret its own interpretive activity. Self-interpretation generates regress. Three exits:

1. **Infinite regress** — a chain hanging from nothing. Explanatorily vacuous.
2. **Brute terminus** — stops at an unexplained fact. Abandons explanation where it matters most.
3. **Self-grounding terminus** — requires internal differentiation: knower, known, and act of knowing as distinct-but-unified.

Formal anchors (structural, not proof):

- Gödel (1931): no consistent system proves its own consistency.
- Tarski (1933): no language defines its own truth predicate.
- Turing (1936): no algorithm decides halting for arbitrary self-referential computation.
