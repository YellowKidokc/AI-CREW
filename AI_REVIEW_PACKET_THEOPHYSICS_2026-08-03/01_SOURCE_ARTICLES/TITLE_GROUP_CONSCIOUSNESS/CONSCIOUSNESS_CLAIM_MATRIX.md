## Register Rules

Theology can say: persons are receivers, witnesses, embodied souls, moral agents, and image-bearers oriented toward God. Christ may be the interpretive center of the model.

Philosophy can say: eliminativism faces a reflexive problem; self-interpretation raises regress pressure; a terminus may require differentiated unity.

Neuroscience/science can say: neural correlates, cognition, behavior, animal evidence, information measures, and AI architectures are live research domains. The hard problem remains open.

Theophysics bridge can say: the framework models consciousness as self-referential, oriented relation and explores how triadic termination, coupling, and grace-language map onto that structure.

Lean-testable can say: conditional dependency theorems, gate definitions, role profiles, regress-exit classifications, and zero-veto analogues can be formalized once stated as definitions or assumptions.

Speculative/quarantined must say: scalar-field derivation of consciousness, Phi threshold certainty, localized consciousness field, binary sign consciousness, and "Cross as fixed point" compression are not public proof claims.
