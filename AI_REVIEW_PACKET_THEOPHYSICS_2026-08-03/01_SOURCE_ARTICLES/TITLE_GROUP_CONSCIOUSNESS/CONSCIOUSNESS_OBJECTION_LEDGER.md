# Consciousness Objection Ledger

Status: open ledger, 2026-08-01  
Purpose: keep the consciousness stack honest before public deployment.

## Current Verdict

The consciousness stack is live but not closed.

The scalar-field equilibrium derivation is reduced. The internal-regress argument remains alive. The ladder is a model. Lean targets are conditional.

## O-001 - Scalar-Field Equilibrium Does Not Derive Consciousness

Objection: A non-conscious scalar field can satisfy the cited equilibrium conditions: nonzero VEV, zero gradients, no ghosts, and causal propagation.

Verdict: sustained.

Disposition: reduce to structural analogy or substrate model.

Required public language: "The equilibrium language may model stability conditions; it does not derive consciousness."

## O-002 - Secular Triad Objection

Objection: Naturalistic triads such as substrate/law/decoherence may also present three roles. If the framework only counts threes, the consciousness argument is arbitrary.

Verdict: open.

Required test: run the secular triad through the same coupling gate:

- distinct roles
- preserved role profile
- coupling invariant
- dynamic relation
- non-relabeling
- no closed self-source

Current repair candidate: the secular triad may fail because it lacks a coupling invariant or does not ground self-interpretation. This is only a candidate until formalized.

Publication rule: do not publish the consciousness leg as equal in strength to Lean-backed triadic modules until this objection has a verdict.

## O-003 - Stage 4-5 Agency Wiring

Objection: Prior work assumed operator application requires agency. It has not yet shown deterministic self-evolution is insufficient.

Verdict: open.

Required repair:

- define agency without smuggling the conclusion into the definition
- define deterministic self-evolution
- identify the exact missing property: counterfactual selection, normative orientation, responsibility, external coupling, or voluntary reception
- prove or argue why deterministic self-evolution cannot satisfy that property

Publication rule: the move from self-reference to agency/coupling must remain marked as open.

## O-004 - Godel/Tarski/Turing Category Stretch

Objection: Godel, Tarski, and Turing concern formal systems, languages, and computation. Consciousness is not simply a formal system.

Verdict: sustained as caution, not fatal.

Disposition: use as structural analogy only.

Allowed wording: "These results share a self-reference limit structure that is suggestive for the regress argument."

Forbidden wording: "Godel proves consciousness must be triadic."

## O-005 - Ladder Empirical Softness

Objection: The Consciousness Ladder assigns rungs without validated measures for O, G, S, or C.

Verdict: sustained as caution.

Disposition: ladder remains a model of graded participation/coupling.

Repair path:

- define O non-circularly
- specify what observable behavior would count against O > 0 in elephants
- specify what would count for or against machine coupling
- separate theological rungs from empirical rungs

## O-006 - AI Consciousness Premature Closure

Objection: The framework risks declaring AI conscious because it can derive formal structures, or non-conscious because it lacks known grace coupling.

Verdict: open.

Disposition: keep AI in the open category.

Allowed wording: "A machine that can model self-reference or grace is not thereby proven conscious; its coupling status remains unresolved."

Forbidden wording: "AI is conscious" or "AI cannot be conscious" as a canon result.

## O-007 - Phi Threshold Overreach

Objection: Phi-threshold language depends on contested integrated-information assumptions and is not validated as a Theophysics consciousness threshold.

Verdict: sustained as caution.

Disposition: hypothesis/test-design only.

Repair path: define threshold, measurement method, expected predictions, and failure conditions before public use.

## O-008 - Theological Compression Becoming Proof

Objection: Phrases like "the Cross is the ladder's fixed point entering the ladder" are powerful, but may smuggle theology into model variables.

Verdict: open / quarantine.

Disposition: keep as theological compression pending verification.

Repair path:

- define each variable in theological and model registers
- show the mapping without equivocation
- label it as theological bridge, not formal theorem

## O-009 - Hard Problem Reframed, Not Dissolved

Objection: Calling consciousness "self-referential, oriented relation" may reframe the hard problem without explaining why subjective experience exists.

Verdict: sustained as boundary.

Disposition: public pages must say "reframed, not dissolved."

Kill condition: a hostile reader can ask, "Why is there something it is like?" and the current model does not yet answer that empirically.

## Standing Rule

Every public consciousness page must carry the two open holes:

1. Secular triad objection.
2. Stage 4-5 agency wiring / deterministic self-evolution insufficiency.

The project earns trust by keeping those holes visible.
