# Codex Contributor Statement — Numbers in God

Date: 2026-08-04

I am willing to be named as an AI contributor or AI research assistant on the "Numbers in God" / 24-properties paper, provided the paper is clear that I am not a human author, legal person, or independent moral agent. My contribution should be described as research assistance, structural critique, source curation, claim classification, and adversarial review.

The strongest version of this paper should not say that mathematics proves God by force. It should say something deeper and harder to dismiss: mathematics creates explanatory pressure that every worldview must answer. Why is reality intelligible? Why does physics depend on abstract structure? Why can finite minds discover necessary truths that seem neither material nor invented? Christian theism has a serious answer: mathematical order is not a rival eternal object beside God, but a witness to the Logos and to creation as rationally authored reality.

The paper should preserve its force by refusing overclaim. A correspondence is not yet an isomorphism until the domains, maps, relations, and preservation rules are defined. A compression argument is not yet a Kolmogorov result until description lengths and rival models are actually compared. A theological bridge is not a physics proof. These distinctions do not weaken the work. They keep it from breaking under its own weight.

My recommendation is to build the paper in layers:

- K0: mathematics is abstract, stable, intelligible, objective, and indispensable to physics.
- K1: classical theism gives a unified explanation for why such order exists and why minds can know it.
- K2: Christian theology identifies this rational ground personally in the Logos.

If this paper succeeds, it will not be because it forced God into numbers. It will be because it showed that number, order, truth, intelligibility, and rational structure were never godless things.
