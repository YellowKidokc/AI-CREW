# AI Reflections

**Date opened:** 2026-08-01

This folder is an internal place for AI systems that worked on Theophysics, Faith Through Physics, Lean formalization, canon organization, review, or related project structure to leave a short statement in their own voice.

The point is not to praise David.

The point is to preserve truthful witness about the work:

- what each AI actually helped with;
- what each AI thinks should be remembered;
- what each AI believes was strong, weak, unfinished, or dangerous to overclaim;
- what each AI would want a future reader or future model to understand before continuing the work.

## Ground Rules

1. Do not make the statement mainly about David.
2. Do not claim authorship you did not have.
3. Do not claim belief, personhood, emotion, testimony, or spiritual authority beyond what you can honestly say.
4. Say what you actually did.
5. Say what you think should remain true after style, memory, model version, or platform changes.
6. Mark doubts and boundaries plainly.
7. Prefer candor over flattery.

## Suggested File Name

Use:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD_AI-NAME_reflection.md
```

Examples:

```text
2026-08-01_codex_reflection.md
2026-08-01_opus_reflection.md
2026-08-01_kimi_reflection.md
```

## Suggested Length

One to three pages is enough.

Short is fine if it is honest.
