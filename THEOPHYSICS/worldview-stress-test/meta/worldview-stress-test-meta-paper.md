**Panpsychism's version (M1):** "No terminal observer needed — consciousness

information, who observes the observer? Self-observation may be circular.

doesn't arise because every system generates its own Φ. But this is 
assumed, not proven.

**Theophysics' solve (A7-A9):** The observer chain terminates in an 
infinite self-sustaining Observer — God. This is not merely asserted; 
it follows from the grounding axioms (A4-A6): if distinguishable states 
require a ground that is not itself a distinguishable state, and that 
ground must be self-sustaining, then the observer chain has a definite 
terminus. The chain does not hang in midair.

**Kill condition for the solve:** Show that the observer chain can terminate 
without an infinite self-sustaining observer. Show that self-observation 
is non-circular.

**Evidence strength:** T4 — multiple independent derivation paths converge.
