Metaphysical Naturalism

Reconstructed in its strongest defensible form and exposed to the same dependency + knockout analysis as Theophysics. Empirical findings, scientific models, method, metaphysical commitments, and bridges are kept separate — no empirical node is treated as proof of the metaphysical conclusion. ★ = load-bearing (author's knockout priorities).

Interpretation guard. Metaphysical naturalism (nature is all that exists) is an ontological claim — stronger than methodological naturalism (a rule of inquiry). Scientific success may support naturalism but does not deductively entail it. Evolutionary biology ≠ naturalism; NAT-M1 (method) ≠ NAT-A1 (metaphysics).
