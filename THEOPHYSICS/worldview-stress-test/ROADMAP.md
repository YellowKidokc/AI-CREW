## Every Graph Answers the Same Five Questions

1. What are your primitives?
2. What are your bridge claims?
3. What are your empirical supports?
4. What are your unresolved burdens?
5. What breaks if a load-bearing node is removed?

## The Goal

Not "here's my worldview" but "here's a comparative atlas of major
explanatory frameworks, all represented with the same dependency
logic, knockout analysis, and evidence standards."

A resource that reviewers — even ones who disagree — can engage
with because the methodology is applied consistently.

## Jim's Point

This doesn't depend on the Lean build finishing. It gives the team
something objective to test against right now. And the Five Walls
paper and the Grace memo already demonstrate the philosophy: invite
structural objections rather than treating conclusions as beyond
criticism. Apply that same standard to every framework in the atlas.
