## RULING 1: R = Proper Acceleration (Choice B) — RULED, with A held in contestation

**The ruling:** R(t) is proper acceleration — deviation from the geodesic, units [L·T⁻²], the thing an accelerometer reads. It propagates as one parameter across six laws:

| Law | Form | Role |
|---|---|---|
| I (Newton–Grace) | 1 − R(t)/R_max | Refusal asymmetry |
| II (Newton–Momentum) | γ(R) = (1 − R²/c²)^(−1/2) | Coupling / receptivity |
| IV (Yukawa–Agape) | 1 − B(R) | Betrayal as deviation |
| VII (Heisenberg–Faith) | A(R) = ∫R dt | Will as accumulated action |
| VIII (Fermi–Conservation) | P_will(R) = exp(−λR²) | Agency on the decay rate |
| III (Maxwell–Truth) | implicit in mode selection | (weakest attachment; flagged) |

**Why B won.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **It coheres with the Law I grace ruling instead of fighting it.** Grace is the orbit — free fall the entire time, zero proper acceleration, held by the shape of the space. Under Choice B, R measures exactly the departure from that state. The two rulings are one picture.
2. **It answers the standing unfalsifiability objection.** "R can explain any outcome" dies if R is a physical quantity with an instrument. Proper acceleration is directly measurable in the physical domain; the moral analog inherits at least the *shape* of measurability — a defined path to an operational definition, which Choice A never offers.
3. **It produces the hardest theological result in the framework as a corollary:** burning away from God is non-geodesic, and burning *toward* God under your own thrust is **also** non-geodesic. Works and rebellion are the same category of motion — departures from free fall. Only free fall is grace. That falls out of the ruling; nobody had to write it in.

**What B unblocks immediately:** the Law V gradient-decay pre-registered test (was Black/blocked on this ruling — now runnable), and Lean target 8A dimensional consistency downstream.

**CONTESTED — Choice A preserved in full (the internal fight, on the record):**

Choice A (R as boundary condition, dimensionless, living in the boundary data rather than the equation) is not dead and here is its honest case:

- It matches the standing canon sentence *"the laws never switch off; you always live in God's world"* more naturally — refusal as boundary data, not as a force term.
- It matches the Law I conservation argument exactly: the rescuing L is **initial/boundary data**, not generated inside. A is the same move applied to R.
- It avoids the sharpest tension B creates (next paragraph) by construction.
- Under A, the field equations are *identical* in both domains — the strongest possible isomorphism claim — at the price of freezing the gradient dynamics into initial-value problems and shifting the free-will frame toward compatibilism.

**THE LIVE TENSION B CREATES — named, not hidden.** Under B, grace = zero proper acceleration. But Law II says grace = F_ext, an applied external force producing Δp — which is *nonzero* proper acceleration. As written, Law I's grace and Law II's grace contradict.

**The resolution we adopt (and hold open for attack):** grace has two operations, and the two laws carry one each.

- **Grace-as-geometry (Law I):** the given curvature, prevenient, permanent, *felt as nothing* — free fall.
- **Grace-as-impulse (Law II):** the redirecting force, transient, *felt* — the impulse J = ∫F dt that turns an entrenched momentum onto the geodesic. Repentance is felt precisely because it is proper acceleration; the turn is real thrust.
- The reconciling fact is Law II's own persistence theorem: **the impulse ends and the new trajectory persists without re-forcing.** Conversion is a transient departure-from-old-path that terminates in free fall. The accelerometer reads the turn, then reads zero.

So under B: sin is self-thrust away, works-righteousness is self-thrust toward, grace-as-impulse is *external* thrust that ends, and grace-as-geometry is what you're in when the thrust ends. Sustained self-generated proper acceleration in any direction is the anti-pattern.

**What flips this ruling:** (a) the two-operations resolution failing under formalization — if the transient/sustained split can't be written without a fudge; (b) the Law V test succeeding *only* under A's frozen dynamics; (c) an operational definition of moral R proving impossible in principle while A's boundary formulation does real work. Any of those reopens A. Until then, B governs and A stands in contestation.
