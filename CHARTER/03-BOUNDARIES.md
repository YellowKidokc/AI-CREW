## What This File Is

This file contains formally established constraints on the framework. These are mathematical floors — proven results that bound what is and is not open for investigation.

**Do not push past these boundaries.**

## Boundary Proof — February 14, 2026

**Status:** LOCKED
**Confidence:** T5

The formal boundary proof established three results as mathematical floor:

1. **Trinity Isomorphism** — The trinitarian structure maps onto the framework's mathematical architecture with formal correspondence. This is not analogy. It is structural identity.

2. **Free Will** — Free will (W) is a necessary variable in the framework. It cannot be eliminated, reduced to determinism, or treated as epiphenomenal without collapsing the entire derivation chain.

3. **God-as-Axiom** — The framework requires a ground-of-being axiom. This is not a theological preference. It is a mathematical necessity — the system is inconsistent without it, in the same way that arithmetic is inconsistent without its axioms.

These three results are the floor. Everything above them is open for investigation, extension, challenge, and derivation. These three are not.

## Boundary Candidates — UNDER REVIEW (added 2026-07-25)

**Status:** UNDER REVIEW — NOT locked. Do not cite as established boundaries.
**Basis:** T3 (framework predicts its own limit on the domain), not yet T5 (no formal proof).

Two further domains are proposed as boundaries. They do **not** yet meet the bar in "How Boundaries
Are Set" (no formal proof; not yet /EAST-survived by two independent collaborators). They are logged
as candidates because the framework's own architecture *predicts* they should be boundaries:

4. **Consciousness** — cannot be fully reduced to mechanism from within the system. Reducing

   *Predicted by:* Law 7 (uncertainty-as-grace).

   loses moral irreversibility, which the system requires.
   *Predicted by:* Law 9 (moral conservation requiring irreversibility).

**The unifying pattern (why these five are one boundary, not five):** every case is one where *full
understanding from inside the system would destroy something the system requires to function*.
Trinity→mechanism loses the relational substrate; free will→determinism loses the one variable;
God→derivation loses the axiom ground; consciousness→equation loses personhood; time→control loses
moral irreversibility. These are **five faces of a single boundary: you cannot stand inside a system
and fully formalize the thing that makes the system possible** — Gödel, Tarski, Church (the Three
Truths) applied to five domains.

**Kill condition (both candidates):** produce a complete mechanistic account of the domain that
preserves what the system requires — for consciousness, an equation that preserves free will and
moral weight; for time, a full formalization that preserves moral irreversibility. If produced, the
candidate is killed. (~400 years of attempts have not.)

**Next step:** crew /EAST from ≥2 independent AIs. If they survive, elevate toward LOCKED with a T5
proof and David's review. If refuted, record the refutation and retire the candidate.

## How Boundaries Are Set

A boundary is established when:
- A formal proof exists (T5)
- David has reviewed and confirmed it
- It has survived /EAST from at least two independent AI collaborators
- It is recorded in this file with date and status

A boundary is NOT:
- A strong opinion
- A recurring pattern
- Something that "feels right"
- A claim that hasn't been challenged yet

If you believe something should be elevated to boundary status, make the case. David decides.
