  can save major reconstruction later.

  not everyone assumes someone else will handle it.

David's field-work analogy:

- When too many people stand around one job, responsibility diffuses.
- With three people, the third person gets the tool the first time because the
  duty is obvious.
- The better pattern is one assigned night per week. That night might be hard,
  and if it is truly bad everyone can help, but most nights the duty is small.
- The rest of the week, people are not hovering over work that is not theirs.

Operational interpretation:

- Do not make every AI watch every pump every night.
- Give each AI a clear rotation/handoff duty.
- Expect the agent to do it without David having to rebuild the whole prompt.
- Keep enough durable record that the next agent can continue.
