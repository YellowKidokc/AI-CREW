## What Looks Right

1. **The biggest correction is healthy:** Level 2 is now a postulated first-order open gradient model over the Level 1 coherence landscape, not "Level 1 differentiated" and not a uniquely forced law of physics. That downgrade makes the model more honest, not weaker.

2. **The units repair is necessary:** reduced factors `X_i`, `chi`, and `grad chi` should be dimensionless; `dX/dt`, `W`, and `eta` should carry `s^-1`. The earlier bits/s product could not survive dimensional analysis. Keeping bits/s only in the Level-0 Shannon parent equations is the cleanest current position.

3. **C_W outside the product is the right guardrail:** Law 10 / coherence should wrap or normalize the nine-factor product. Treating C as a tenth factor created dictionary drift and confused the zero-veto product with the coherence operator.

4. **The weak eta theorem is a good locked core:** if the gradient is zero, mobility kills the zero gradient, and `eta` is nonzero, instantaneous velocity is nonzero. That is modest, true within the declared formal system, and worth keeping. It does not prove grace, global convergence, or salvation mechanics.

5. **The retest protocol has the right shape:** version-drift intake first; canonical state validation; gradient algebra; source-term behavior; mobility operator; boundary behavior; field-to-reduced consistency; then Law 5 and Law 9 reproduction. This is exactly the order that prevents old results from being smuggled into the new structure.

## What Still Worries Me

1. **Law 5 is the first real kill gate.** Pure ascent on a product coherence landscape is coherence-nondecreasing when `W >= 0`. So if Law 5 requires actual decay, the decay must enter through a coordinate definition, damping term, transformed potential, sector-specific reduction, negative/non-PSD mobility, boundary behavior, or `eta`. If the model adds a decay term only after seeing the target, the gradient structure fails that gate.

2. **Law 9 Gamma_sin is equally load-bearing.** The model must reproduce the directional/asymmetric moral-conservation signature without arbitrary tuning. If a shuffled dictionary, random W, or anti-Lagrangian control performs just as well, the mapping is not carrying real weight.

3. **W is currently too underspecified.** Calling `W` free will is bridge language until its functional form is ruled. At minimum, the model needs to say whether W is scalar, diagonal, matrix-valued, positive semidefinite, state-dependent, agency-offset-coupled, or allowed to create drift against the gradient.

4. **eta is also underspecified.** The source term can be constant, scheduled, stochastic, state-dependent, targeted by syndrome, or boundary-projected. Those are not equivalent. The model should not be allowed to pass tests with arbitrary eta and then interpret that freedom as grace.

5. **[0,1]^9 invariance is not optional forever.** If the state space is normalized spiritual/relational coordinates, the dynamics must keep trajectories inside the admissible cube or define the projection/reparameterization that does. Clipping, reflecting boundaries, logistic coordinates, and barrier terms can change the behavior and must be tested separately.

6. **The active bulletin still reads hotter than the ruling in places.** It says "key properties 90% confidence" and "annealing theorem 95%+" in a way that could be mistaken for canonical proof. The ruling correctly downgrades global annealing to an open theorem. The bulletin should keep the urgency but mirror the ruling's precision.

7. **The Lagrangian bridge remains open.** The reduced gradient flow, older first-order `dchi/dt = G - S + Gamma`, and second-order field/Lagrangian forms must be related by an explicit limit, projection, or sector boundary. If they govern the same object differently, that is a fork.

8. **L_D may be the missing negative-side object.** The denominator-only adversary and syndrome/eigenmode discussion point toward a separate discoherence operator. That may be the honest home for decay modes instead of forcing the positive gradient term to do both ascent and decay.
